# FAQs for welcome meeting

What is a Welcome Meeting? The welcome meeting is the first meeting post hosting of a new service. It is a process of educating and hand-holding our newly on-boarded customers/ services in person by FSF team.Why Welcome Meeting?IndiaMART wants to excel and exceed in creating Customer Delight. As a step towards that, we would now start educating and setting correct expectations for our onboarded Suppliers.When do we need to do a Welcome Meeting? New Service Hosted  >>  Welcome CallPost 3 Days of Welcome Call Disposition >> Welcome Meeting is Assigned   (TAT for Completion = 3Days) We select ‘Welcome Meeting’ as the purpose of MERP.Do not meet the client immediately on a case assignment. Wait until the Welcome Meeting is opened. Where can I check for meetings pending at my/ my team’s level?WebERP > Services > Sales MIS > Welcome MeetingWhat do we educate Supplier in Welcome Meeting?Filter Buy Leads as per requirement and Consume (From App)Check Messages and Reply (From App)Receive PNS Calls from IndiaMART BuyersWhat I will get if I do Welcome Meeting?Also since the educated customer would remain longer in the system, you’d also increase the chance of an upsell to that particular clientWhat if I didn’t complete the Welcome Meeting within Timelines?You would lose out on Incentive and Meeting Count.What if I am unable to answer a particular query from customer during the service meeting?It can also be done from WebERP > Company > Search STS > STS > Open Ticket.How I will be able to enter the meeting in MERP?Select STS > Purpose > Choose “Welcome Meeting” from dropdownHow would I dispose of my Welcome Meetings?Once you have educated and made the Supplier complete the 4 activities the status of the meeting would automatically change from “Pending” to “Completed”.
