# MCAT Ranking Definition and Improved MCAT Evaluation Logic

This article explains how MCAT (Market Category) ranks are assigned, what each rank means, and how the A-Rank distribution framework ensures fair visibility across all seller types.1. What Is MCAT Ranking?MCAT ranking determines a seller's visibility in BuyLeads, BizFeeds, and catalogue-based discovery. Ranks are calculated using:Seller transaction historyProduct quality & mapping recencyNegative-interaction to transaction ratio (NI/Txn)Possible ranks are: A, B, BA, BB, C, CA, CB, CC, D, DA, DB.2. A-Rank Distribution FrameworkWhy Was This Introduced?Historical data showed Catalog and TSCatalog sellers holding a disproportionately large number of A-rank MCATs, leading to uneven enquiry distribution and reduced competitiveness. The new framework resolves this by capping A-rank MCAT counts per seller type.2.1 Maximum Allowed A-Rank MCATs by Seller TypeSeller TypeMax A-Rank MCATsCatalog7TS Catalog17Star27Leader37Preferred Leader / Industry LeaderNo limitIndiaMart Verified ExportersNo limitCatalog sellers within first 30 days of joiningNo limit2.2 Special Handling RulesNew Sellers (Client Age < 1 Month)No restriction on A-rank MCAT count.All Non-Name-Only MCATs are automatically assigned A-rank.Default SellersThe limit applied is based on the seller's original category before they moved to Default.2.3 Prioritization When Eligible MCATs Exceed the LimitTop A-rank MCATs are selected based on:Total transactions in the last 14 daysMost recent BuyLead / BizFeed purchase activityℹ️ MCATs that exceed the allowed limit are downgraded to BA-Rank, not removed.3.
