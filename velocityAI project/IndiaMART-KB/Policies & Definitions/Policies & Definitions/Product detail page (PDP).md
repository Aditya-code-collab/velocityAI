# Product detail page (PDP)

What is PDP?PDP is a Product Detail Page which displays detailed information of any specific product such as Product Name, Product Price, Description and Image of the product.Buyers can visit Product Detail Page of the product in which he is interested in and can post an enquiry to the supplier via various CTA’s present on the page such as ‘Contact Seller’, ‘Get Latest Price’, ‘Send Email’ and ‘Send SMS’. This page can be accessed via clicking on Product Name link from any page of Indiamart such as HomePage, DIR on which different products of suppliers are listed.Furthermore, we have different sections on PDP page through which more product’s PDP pages can be explored.View All productsIn this section, we display products from the same seller as well as related products from different sellers. Product Details and Company DetailsIn this section, Detailed description and any technical specification of the product is shownIn company details, we show company information such as Nature of Business, Year of establishment, turnover of the company and other information about the company. A company video is also shown in this section. Compare similar products from other sellersDifferent products can be compared with similar specifications.Browse related categoriesThis section shows different categories related to the product. Example if PDP page of a laptop is being viewed, then related categories shown will be Laptops, Dell Laptops, Desktop Computers etc.Example Link:https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/hp-probook-650g1-20672555988.htmlHow we are benefited?The Product Detail page is the main resource of Indiamart where users can look and compare detailed information of different products and can send enquiries to the supplier of that product, therefore, fulfilling the needs of the buyer as well as supplier.These pages are also indexed on Google. The PDP link is displayed like https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/hp-probook-650g1-20672555988.html Conditions to be fulfilled for making PDP page liveProduct’s approval status (Approved, Modified or Out of stock) Seller’s Country = India or If seller’s Country= Foreign, then Seller should be having any paid services of IndiamartSeller’s listing status = LST (Listed) or TFL (To be free listed)The seller should not be marked as disabled.Name, description and Image should exist for that productDescription of the product should be greater than 100 characters.If a Product description is the same as product name, then count of ISQ (Product Specification) should be greater than or equal to 3.If the product description and product name are different then the count of ISQ (Product Specification) should be greater than or equal to one.
