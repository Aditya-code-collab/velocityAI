# NACH bounce policy for 0-30 days tenure client.

We will download the 0 NACH cleared customer if 2 consecutive Auto NACH hits get bounced. Such cases will be downloaded within 2 days of post receiving clearance status of 2nd hit.Example: If a client is hosted on 3rd Dec’18, his First Auto hit will be scheduled on 5th Dec’18 and second Auto hit will be on 10th Dec’18 presuming both the hits are bounce, we will download this client within 2 days post the 2nd NACH hit i.e. 10th Dec’18.Note: If customer wants to enroll again in paid services, then he would be eligible for annual mode only.
