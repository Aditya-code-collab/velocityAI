# BuyLead reversal process

As per the current process, BuyLead credits are auto reversed for the first 6 requests subject to a time duration limit of 6 reversals in last 365 days for that GLID from that particular request.In case client request reversal post 6 leads then a ticket will get auto-locked for corresponding GLID and account managers will evaluate the request at their level.Account managers will evaluate that reversal request and previous credit reversal request pattern to check if the request is genuine or not. In case the request does not seems genuine then the account managers will handle the objection at their level and in the case where the request is genuine then an internal ticket will be locked in BuyLead Approval / Mapping issuesPath to raise the ticket:STS >> Select the external ticket >> Open an internal ticket >> Select BuyLead Approval / Mapping issues bucketPrerequisites for opening an Internal Ticket in BuyLead Approval / Mapping issues bucket under internal queries:1.  Must Contain: - Supplier GLID: as in which supplier is facing the issue?- Offer ID: The BuyLead in which issue is being faced- Specific Issue the supplier is facing (specifying the exact reason for which the supplier has raised a complaint/supplier VOC) 2. Must Not Contain: - Concerns having texts as Fake Buy leads, Irrelevant Leads- Screenshots of Offer ID, buyer details, etc.Note: Final decision for reversal of the 7 and further BuyLeads will be taken by Leap team with VOC team’s intervention basis the findings.
