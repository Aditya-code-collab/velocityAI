# What if I don't find the right opportunity for a job at IndiaMART immediately?

If you do not see your ideal next opportunity, simply submit your candidate profile. The candidate profile provides us with a summary of your skills and experiences and enters you into our applicant database. When a position becomes available that matches your criteria, you will receive an e-mail notification. Please visit https://careers.indiamart.com/jobs/ to submit your candidate profile or share your CV at talentacquisition@indiamart.com.
