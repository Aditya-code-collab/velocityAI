# Create FCP/ Free seller account/ Register at IndiaMART

This will take you to the dashboard  (seller.indiamart.com) where you can start adding products to build your catalog.The client can access IndiaMART through our website and mobile app.Please note that it may take up to 7 days to free list your company.
