# How seller can initiate verification call for FCP?

We have implemented a new 'Click to call' widget on the seller dashboard, that allows a qgFCP cust type free seller to initiate a verification call, which if completed successfully, will upgrade his cust type to vgFCP. When the customer clicks on the CTA, a call is placed to the call centre at Vkalp and when the executive is online, the customer will receive a call on his primary mobile number and the conversation to verify his catalog happens.What has been done now?We have placed the 'Click 2 Call' widget on the first fold of dashboard which will say that the call verification is pending for the supplier's catalog, along with a small text label on how to call verification will help him improve his presence on IndiaMART. The 'Request a Call' CTA on the right of the widget allows the user to place the call, on clicking of which we show a countdown below the widget showing an approx time in which the call will be placed, followed by a confirmation message that the call has been successfully placed to his primary phone number.     We hide the widget for the user for the rest of the day after he has placed the call (since cust type will be updated to vgFCP overnight). The next day if his cust type is still qgFCP, he will again be shown the widget. Also, the widget will be hidden during non-office hours (office hours - 10 AM to 7 PM) and on Sundays, when the inbound centre will not be functional.
