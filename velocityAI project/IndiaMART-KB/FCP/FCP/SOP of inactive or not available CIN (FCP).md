# SOP of inactive or not available CIN (FCP)

Incase on CIN not found or not active the clients' listing has been removed. In order to enable the listing associate has to take the following action:Initially check Disable history remark mentioned in Glusr historyIf it is mentioned like, “CIN Not found or CIN status is Not Active”  then Glusr’s FCP has been disabled because of not having an “Active CIN” in Glusr profile.Here FCP would be Disabled and Glusr would be active as a buyer(Note: For a Private Limited Company having an Active CIN number is Mandatory)History Comment View:
