# Enable STS (NFL/ DNF/ Disable) or remove NFL/ DNF/ Disable

How to Remove NFL-Internal request only-We check for the four mandatory field's existence in Gluser, i. e. Company Name, Primary Email Id, Primary Mobile Number & Address, if any of the fields are missing then we suggest the Sales CC get it updated from Client's end & as soon as the client update the detail, we approve to free list (TFL) the GLUSR.How to Remove DNF-Internal Request only- a) If the DNF Gluser marked as a child & parent STS is free listed, then we suggest the sales CC follow the parent STS but if the parent STS have wrong details i.e. Not Accessible/Ex emp. details etc. then we enable the DNF profile and swap the parent-child linking  b) If none of the parent-child relationships exists, then we enable the Gluser & confirm the same to the Sales CC.How to Remove Disable/ activate the account-If disabled reason - "User don't want to be listed"Internal Request-We suggest the Sales CC share confirmation mail from client's primary e-mail id for activation, as soon as we receive the same, we free list the GluserExternal Request-If disabled Reason - "User don't want to be listed"Confirmation mail or call from client's primary e-mail id/ mobile is enough to free list the Gluserif disabled Reason - "Primary Mobile claimed in another Gluser"Internal Request-We suggest the Sales CC share a Confirmation Mailer along with GST from Client's primary mail ID for activation, as soon as we receive the same, we free list the GLUSRExternal Request-Confirmation Mail or call (GST when Pvt ltd Comp.) from Client's primary mail ID/ phone is enough to free list the GLUSR Please NoteTo enable the company CIN and GST both are mandatory to be approved/verified first.In another word, a private limited/ limited/ LLP can't have a live FCP without verified GST and CIN.
