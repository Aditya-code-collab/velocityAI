# Why Credit Note and Tax Invoice both are generated for the same amount?

Tax Invoice is generated when payment is cleared and the deal is marked as "Okay" by OVP team.If any corrections are requested in Tax Invoice, then a new Supplementary Tax Invoice is created post corrections.Since the Supplementary invoice is created on the current date (backdated invoice cannot be created). Credit Note for the old invoice is created to balance the payments made.Note: Client can claim GST on new Supplementary Tax Invoice.
