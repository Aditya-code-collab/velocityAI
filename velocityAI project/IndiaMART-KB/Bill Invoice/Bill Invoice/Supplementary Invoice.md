# Supplementary Invoice

Supplementary Invoice gets generated in the below scenarios:1.      Adjustment cases2.      Amendment in Invoices due to changes in company details or GST Number.Please note:Supplementary Invoice being sent to the client, after changes are done TAT of sending Supplementary Invoice is same as for actual invoices, as they are also tagging based invoices i.e. 24 hoursAs Government doesn't allow to amend any invoices before 31st March 2019 so we cannot amend this invoice.Do we send Credit Notes to the client?Yes, we do send credit notes to the customer on his registered email id and being reflected on seller panel as well, be it for ledger settlement, amendments in invoices.The path for the invoice/ credit notes :Seller.IM >> IndiaMART Invoices >> Invoices/Credit Notes >> Print/DownloadPrerequisites for a supplementary invoice-Need original invoiceIf any modification is required then initially changes need to be done in WebERP then the same needs to be updated in STSAfter that, initiate a ticket under Supplementary Invoice - Finance
