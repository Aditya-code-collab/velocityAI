# Prerequisites for WO Re-issuance (MDC Only) internal ticket

Mentioned below are the prerequisites to be taken care/followed by the sales team before raising a WO Re-issuance (MDC Only ticket) in the internal ticket bucket:Ticket to be raised within one working day from the work order cancellation and if the ticket is not raised within this duration, the proforma will be cancelled.If the case is found "ok" by the production team, the work order will be re-issued within one working day.However, if the case is still "not ok" then the work order will be cancelled and the ticket will be closed. Before opening the ticket, sales team need to ensure that the reason for cancellation is resolved.Cases wherein work order has been cancelled more than twice then the work order cannot be reissued even after re-issuance tickets.Post ticket for re-issuance is received CM/VM of the concerned production centre approves re-issuance of the work order.
