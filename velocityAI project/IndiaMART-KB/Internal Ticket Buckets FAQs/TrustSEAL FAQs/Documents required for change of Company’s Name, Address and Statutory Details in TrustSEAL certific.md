# Documents required for change of Company’s Name, Address and Statutory Details in TrustSEAL certificate

Servicing/CC team must attach support documents basis the specific call driver requested by the Supplier, at the time of opening the internal ticket for changes along with the brief reason for the changes else the ticket will be closed as invalid.Ticket Bucket Name: GST/ IEC Change Request (GST/ IEC Verified)1. Change of Company Name:       - Proprietorship Firm:  · PAN Card Copy          · Copy of GST registration certificate (Existing and the one which needs to be updated)          · Company Co-relation letter (on old company’s letterhead)        - Partnership Firm:  · PAN Card Copy          · Copy of GST registration certificate (Existing and the one which needs to be updated)          · Company Co-relation letter (on old company’s letterhead)          · Partnership Deed copy        -  Public & Private Limited Company:  · PAN Card Copy          · Copy of GST registration certificate (Existing and the one which needs to be updated)          · Company Co-relation letter (on old company’s letterhead)          · Certificate of Incorporation Copy2. Address Change        -  Address change is only possible if it is mentioned in the same GST certificate or in amended                GST certificate        -  Any other dosuments like electricity bill, water bill or rent agreement are not valid    3. Statutory Details Change:        -  Turnover Change:          · Profit & Loss Statement (CA Verified)       -   Quality & Standard, Membership Certificates like- ISO & Export Promotion Council:              · Supportive Certifications required from Authorized Agency
