# Steps for TrustSEAL verification process

Post the sale is locked and payment is received from the customer, finance team issues a Work order for TrustSEAL team.(Note: Cases wherein the ticket is locked “before” or “without work order issuance” will be closed as invalid)1. Post-Work order issuance TS team forward the details to the third-party vendor to schedule the appointment to get documents verified.2. Third-Party take an appointment and visit the Client's place to collect information and documents. 3. Cases wherein the client are not supporting for verification:Cases wherein third party vendor face any challenge in verification it gets escalated to TS TeamPost that TS Team escalates the case to Servicing/CC teamStill, if the client does not support the third-party vendor to perform the verification, we proceed with the cancellation of the work order & download the TS Service4. A third-party vendor prepares a report with the verified documents and sends them to the TrustSEAL team. (TAT for this entire process is 15 days from the date of the case scheduled)5. TrustSEAL team now schedules it for Hosting.6. TrustSEAL team dispatch and deliver the certificate (Hard copy- Within 21 Days after TS Hosting) as the entire process comprise of below mentioned three sub-processesCertificate Printing ScheduledPrint & PackingPickup for Dispatch
