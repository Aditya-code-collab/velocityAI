# What are the mandatory documents for TrustSEAL verification?

Mentioned below is the list of the documents required by the Verification team to perform TrustSEAL verification:Entity Specific Documents:   - For Proprietorship Firm:                 · PAN Card Copy                 · Copy of GST registration certificate     - For Partnership Firm:                 · PAN Card Copy                 · Copy of GST registration certificate                 · Partnership Deed copy     - For Public & Private Limited Company:                  · PAN Card Copy                  · Copy of GST registration certificate                  · Certificate of Incorporation Copy2. Address Proof (Any one of the below mentioned Documents):      ·  Telephone Bill Copy      ·  Electricity Bill Copy      ·  Rent Agreement Copy  3. Statutory Details:     - Turnover:                    · Profit & Loss Statement (CA Verified)     - Quality & Standard, Membership Certificates like- ISO & Export Promotion Council                    ·  Supportive Certifications required from Authorized Agency
