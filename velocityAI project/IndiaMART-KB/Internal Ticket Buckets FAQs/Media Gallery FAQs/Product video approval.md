# Product video approval

There is an approval system for product video which ideally takes 24 hours to reflect on the catalog.Seller Panel >> Product >> Active Product If the video is approved, it will automatically be displayed on the catalog.If rejected, the customer will receive a rejection email with the reason for rejection, and the rejected item will not be visible in the catalog.If still there is an issue you can contact Chittresh Lohani (chittresh.lohani@indiamart.com)
