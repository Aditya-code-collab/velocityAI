# Prerequisites to raise a NACH Adhoc ticket in the internal bucket

Mentioned below are the prerequisites to be followed before raising a NACH Adhoc ticket in an internal bucket: NACH Adhoc post 4 bounces (3 bounces plus 1 adhoc which can be actual activate or adhoc) for welcome cases will not be entertained.NACH Adhoc post 5 bounces (3 bounces plus 2 adhoc which can be actual activate or adhoc) for servicing cases will not be entertained.It is mandatory for the teams to raise the ticket for adhoc NACH presentation with BM’s approval and client’s call recording. Adhoc NACH presentation request can be given to accounts team 1 day prior to the NACH hit date, maximum by 2:00 PM.Example: For all the NACH hits scheduled for 25th Dec’17, the business team can share Adhoc request by 22nd Dec'17 which is the latest working day before 25th Hit. All such Adhoc NACH presentation request should be received by the finance team before 2 PM, on 22nd Dec'17.For all the hits scheduled for Monday, the request should be submitted before 2 pm on Friday.
