# In which bucket NACH Adhoc & Monthly Mode Enquiry tickets to be initiated?

Mentioned below are the bucket selection prerequisites for NACH Adhoc & Monthly Mode Enquiry tickets:If the concern is for NACH Activation then the ticket is to be opened in “Monthly Mode Enquiry” Bucket.If the concern is for NACH Adhoc then the ticket is to be opened in “NACH Adhoc” Bucket.If the concern is for NACH Activation and NACH Adhoc both then a single ticket is to be opened in “NACH Adhoc bucket”.
