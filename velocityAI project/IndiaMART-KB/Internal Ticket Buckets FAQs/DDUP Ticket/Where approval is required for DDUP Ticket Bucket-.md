# Where approval is required for DDUP Ticket Bucket?

Cases wherein two STS of the same entity are assigned to two different associates from same vertical and their RM or BM or ZM is same then for Parent marking they have to get the prior approval from respective RM or BM or ZM whomsoever is common in the reporting while raising the ticket.(However, if even the vertical is different then there is no approval required, the final decision will be taken by the Bucket Owner)
