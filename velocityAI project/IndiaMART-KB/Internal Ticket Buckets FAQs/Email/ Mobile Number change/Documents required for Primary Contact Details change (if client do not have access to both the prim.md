# Documents required for Primary Contact Details change (if client do not have access to both the primary details- Email and Mobile number)

Mentioned below are the entity level documents for Primary Contact Details change (if client do not have access to both the primary details- Email and Mobile number):1. Proprietorship Firm: Copy of GST registration certificate + Current Bank Account statement for 6 months                                                Or(Business Aadhaar/Shop Act License) Both business & Owner Name should be present                                                Or(Electricity Bill / Water bill / any Government Tax Receipt) in business name & owner name                                                Or(Registered Rent Agreement) in Business Name & owner name In case, Proprietorship firm has appointed a Manager, then the team would need below mentioned extra documents:                                         - Application on letter head to provide access to new person, signed by authorized signatories   (Authorized signatory details should match with GST details or bank account details)            - PAN of Signatory                                                                                   2. Partnership/LLP:Copy of GST registration certificate- GST should be in firms name only (LLP number Certificate is mandate)                                       +Registered Partnership Deed with Business name/partners name                                      Or(Current Bank Account statement for 6 months)                                      Or (Shop Act License / Electricity Bill / Water bill / any Government Tax Receipt) in business name                                      Or (Registered Rent Agreement) in Business Name In case, Proprietorship firm has appointed a Manager, then the team would need below mentioned extra documents:   - Application on letter head to provide access to new person, signed by authorized signatories   (Authorized signatory details should match with GST details or bank account details)- PAN of Signatory- PAN of Organization 3. Private/Public Ltd:Copy of GST registration certificate + CIN number is mandate                            +Shop Act License in business name                            Or Registered Rent Agreement in Business Name                           Or Electricity Bill/Water Bill/Any government Tax Receipt in business name    In case, Proprietorship firm has appointed a Manager, then the team would need below mentioned extra documents:   - Application on Company's letter head to provide access to new person, signed by authorized signatories (authorized signatory details should match with GST details or bank account details)- PAN of Signatory- PAN of OrganizationNote: Additionally probe the brief reason for changes from the client and update same on the STS.
