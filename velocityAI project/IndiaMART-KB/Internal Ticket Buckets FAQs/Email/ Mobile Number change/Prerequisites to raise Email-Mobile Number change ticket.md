# Prerequisites to raise Email/Mobile Number change ticket

If the client cannot access their primary number, then initiate a ticket in "Email/Mobile Number change" within the Customer Oriented Internal Queries section, including the prerequisite information. Below are the scenarios: a: Primary email is accessible.The process SOP for KYC (Email/Mobile Number change) team would be as below:Step 1 : CC team should attach email sent by the client with the reason of change and requested new details + upload KYC docs in the STS + Forward the client's email to KYC team with attachments. Mail should come from the primary registered email idStep 2 : KYC team will validate all the docs & once validated will make the required changes & will close the ticket  mentioning cc to get the new contact details marked as "Verified".b: Primary email is not accessible.Step 1 : CC team should attach email sent by the client with the reason of : change and requested new details + upload KYC docs in the STS + Forward the client's email to KYC team with attachments. Mail should come from the email which client want to add as a new email now.Step 2 : KYC team will validate all the docs & once validated will make the required changes & will close the ticket  mentioning cc to  get the new contact details marked as "Verified".Below are the documents required from various types of firms:a: If the primary email is accessible.Proprietorship firmGST is mandatePartnership / LLP FirmGST in firm's name is mandate (If LLIP type firm, LLP No Certificate is mandate)+Consent on the company's letterhead from all partners to change the contact details.Private / Public LtdGST certificate is mandate & CIN certificate is Mandateb: If Primary email is not accessible.Proprietorship firmGST is mandate +Current Bank Account statement for 6 monthsORCancelled Cheque with the business name. OR(Business Aadhaar / Shop Act Licence) both the business name & owner name should be present. OR(Electricity Bill / Water bill / any Govt Tax Receipt) with the business name.  & owner name ORRegistered Rent Agreement with the business & owner name Partnership / LLP FirmGST in firm's name is mandate (If LLIP type firm, LLP No Certificate is mandate)+Consent on the company's letterhead from all partners to change the contact details.+Registered Partnership Deed with the business name or partners' names.ORCurrent Bank Account statement for 6 monthsORCancelled Cheque with the business name.OR(Business Aadhaar/ Shop Act License / Electricity Bill / Water bill / any Govt Tax Receipt) on business nameORRegistered Rent Agreement in Business NamePrivate / Public LtdGST certificate is mandate & CIN certificate is mandate+Shop Act License / Business Aadhaar on business nameORCancelled cheque with the business name.ORRegistered Rent Agreement with the business name.ORElectricity Bill / Water bill / any Govt Tax Receipt with the business name.In case, Company has appointed a new Manager, then would also require below information:a) Application on Company's letter head to provide access to new person, signed by authorized signatories(authorized signatory details should match with GST details or bank account details)b) PAN of signatoryc) PAN of organizationRequested E-mail ID already exist: Sales & Servicing team to get the requested email id removed from another STS and to guide the client for DIY.
