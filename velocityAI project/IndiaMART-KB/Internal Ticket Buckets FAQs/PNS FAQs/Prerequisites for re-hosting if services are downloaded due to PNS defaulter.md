# Prerequisites for re-hosting if services are downloaded due to PNS defaulter

Mentioned below are the scenario wise, required details:1. Recently downloaded case: Prior approval from respective HOD and client’s acknowledgement/ confirmation email is required in case of recently downloaded services irrespective of several defaults marked.2. In the case wherein services are not recently downloaded then:- In case three or more than three defaults are marked than the HOD approval along with the client’s acknowledgement/confirmation email will be required.- For lesser than three defaults just client’s acknowledgement/confirmation email will be required. 3. In case, the seller is a PNS defaulter we will only perform the test call if the customer has not received any call-in last 7 days.
