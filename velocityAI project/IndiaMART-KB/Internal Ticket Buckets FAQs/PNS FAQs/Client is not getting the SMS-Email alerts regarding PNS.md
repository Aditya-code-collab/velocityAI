# Client is not getting the SMS/Email alerts regarding PNS

In case of SMS:Check client's Privacy Setting, if Disabled then asks him to enable the SMS service by logging in to the Seller Account.     Check if the client has blocked to receive the SMS from India Mart, if yes and then ask the client to remove it from the blacklist.Confirm call timings with the client, usually, SMS is delivered within 15 minutes once the call has been completed. In case of Email:Confirm with the client whether the E- mailbox has enough space for new e-mails, if Not then ask the client to update the email-id.Check if the updated email-id is valid, if not, then ask the client to update a valid email id.Confirm if the client has marked the email-id ‘call-support@indiamart.com’ as SPAM, if yes then ask the client to remove it from SPAM to receive PNS alert emails directly to the Inbox.Confirm from the client whether he is talking about successful PNS calls/ missed PNS calls. As from 28 May'20, we will not be sending any emails to our clients after a successful PNS (Preferred number service) call. However, they will continue to receive app notifications for the same. Mail subject: Business Enquiry by Phone on ‘DD: MM: YYYY’ at ‘HH: MM (AM/PM)’(Note: Gl User history to be checked to validate changes made by the seller in above-mentioned cases)Please NoteIf still the issue is not resolved, then initiate a ticket in PNS bucket
