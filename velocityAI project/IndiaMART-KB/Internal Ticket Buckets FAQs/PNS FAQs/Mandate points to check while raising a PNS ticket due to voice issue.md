# Mandate points to check while raising a PNS ticket due to voice issue

In case, a customer is complaining about voice quality-related issues with the PNS number (Preferred number service), it is a mandate for the servicing team to ask operator details and network connectivity in their respective area/areas. Additionally, the servicing team needs to mention the call details wherein the customer has faced the voice quality issue.
