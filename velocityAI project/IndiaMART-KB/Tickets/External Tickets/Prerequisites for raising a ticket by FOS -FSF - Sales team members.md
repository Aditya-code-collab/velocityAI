# Prerequisites for raising a ticket by FOS /FSF / Sales team members

Following points need to be taken care by FSF team while raising a ticket to ensure timely resolution:No vague terms need to be used. For example: Bl irrelevant, PNS call issue, Maturity/ ROI issueConcern should clearly mention the exact issue being faced by the clientFor Example:Instead of just mentioning “Client is facing Irrelevant BL Issued”One should mention what does it mean by irrelevant BL? Such as Product, location, specification etc.Similarly if “Client is facing PNS issue”- What type of PNS issue? voice not clear/ connectivity issue/ calls not receiving etc.Maturity/ ROI issue - Why the client is not receiving Maturity/ ROI?
