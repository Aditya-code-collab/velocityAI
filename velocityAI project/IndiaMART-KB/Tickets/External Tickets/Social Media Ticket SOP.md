# Social Media Ticket SOP

Social media team will initiate first contact with the customer within 15min of a post and pacify the customer.Simultaneously an email thread will be initiated, along with opening a ticket in WebERP under the "Social Media" bucket.The concerned L3 must acknowledge the thread within 1 hour and contact the customer for the resolution.In case of no connect, Email and SMS/Whatsapp to be sent to the customer.Ticket resolution should be provided on the same day, If any approvals or findings are required, the L3 must share the timeline with the client and ensure regular follow-up.L3/L4 to immediately connect with the respective HoDs for relevant approvals so that resolution can be speed up.Ticket closure is permitted only after a complete resolution.Once the resolution is achieved, the L3 must request post deletion from the client.In the absence of an L3, the responsibility falls to the L4 to take the call as L1 or L2 is not permissible to handle the social media escalation.Failure to attempt resolution will fall in severity compliance.Thread closure to be done post RCA from the CC team on the reason of posting on social media.
