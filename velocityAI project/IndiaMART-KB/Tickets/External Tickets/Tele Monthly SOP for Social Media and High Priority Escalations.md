# Tele Monthly SOP for Social Media and High Priority Escalations

Escalations received via Social Media1.1: The Social Media Team will take the call, pacify the client over the call & will initiate a ticket to stop the NACH, the same will be communicated to the client. The deactivation ticket will be kept open for the servicing team in order to attempt retention. 1.2: In case the escalation is received 1-day prior NACH date then the SM team will initiate a Deactivation ticket along with a Monthly mode ticket to stop the NACH looping NACH team, here the NACH team will stop the NACH. Concerned person: Harjeet Singh harjeet.singh@indiamart.comThe deactivation ticket will be kept open for the servicing team in order to retain the client. Escalations received via High Priority: (Append Emails/DA/DG/Legal Escalations/ Board Escalations)2.1: The SM team will take the call and intimate the client that we are arranging a call back by the concerned team ASAP.2.2: The SM team will take the call and pacify the client, and in case the client is adamant to stop the NACH, we will be processing NACH Stop. The ticket will be kept open for retention.2.3: In case the escalation is received 1-day prior NACH date and the client is adamant to stop the NACH then the SM team will initiate a Deactivation ticket along with a Monthly mode ticket to stop the NACH looping NACH team, here the NACH team will stop the NACH. Concerned person: Harjeet Singh harjeet.singh@indiamart.comThe deactivation ticket will be kept open, for the servicing team in order to retain the client.For example, if the Client’s NACH date is the 5th of every month & the NACH escalations is received on the 4th then we will be following scenario 2.3For any other queries and issues, the SM team will initiate a thread with the concerned BM and follow up through emails. In case the NACH is already stopped then the SM team will inform the client and close the ticket.
