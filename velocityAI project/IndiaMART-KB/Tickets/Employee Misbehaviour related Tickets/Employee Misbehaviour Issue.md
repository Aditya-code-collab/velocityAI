# Employee Misbehaviour Issue

If we receive a complaint of an employee regarding Mis-behavior/Rude then need to do the following:Customer Care:The ticket needs to be initiatedSuch tickets are tagged under "Employee Issue" bucket in case of "Paid customers""Free Customers" will go under "Sales and service" bucket.Prerequisites of raising such tickets:Make sure that the below details are taken from the customer(complainant):Full Name of the employee who misbehaved or was rudeContact number (if available)Servicing Team:Discuss with the senior manager and initiate a mail thread for the same.
