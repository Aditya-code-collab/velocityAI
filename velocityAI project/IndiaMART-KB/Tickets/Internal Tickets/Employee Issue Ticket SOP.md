# Employee Issue Ticket SOP

All Employee issue tickets needs to be addressed within 1 hr of its issuance by L3 only. (C2C call is mandatory here)Detailed identification of problem & proper resolution needs to be provided to client as per below mentioned scenarios.After providing proper resolution to client “Always ask for permission to closed” &promote call closing script to capture client’s CLMNote: If team(L3) fails to update VOC for Severity issuance it will counted as FatalL3 is required to fill following details on STS during closure RCA.Client’s issue in detail along with initial RCA: -Name of employee with emp code & dept against which client raised complaint: -Resolution Provided in detail as per below scenarios: -Misbehavior Related:Apologies on the behalf of employee & ensure client that action has been taken against employee & same will not happen again.Mis commitment Related: Empathize the client & set his correct expectations.Product Related: Told client that IM can allow only 400 products addition & promote DIY regarding product addition etc.Excessive Follow-up Renewal/Upsell: Apologies for the same & assure client that now he will not receive excessive calls regarding same.Account Manager Not responding: Try to figure out since how long client facing such issue & apologizes for same afterwards assure client that same thing will not repeat & try to arrange concall/Meeting with req AM then & there.Others: For all others issue on which client’s work/concern is pending should be addressed accordingly & complete work needs to be done.360 checklists followed (Yes or No): -Case escalates to VOC SPOC for severity issuance: -Permission to close taken: -CLM script followed: -All Such tickets are closed by L3 & above only.
