# Outstanding & Hot leads Ticket Handling SOP

Outstanding TicketsAll OS tickets should be touched on the same day of issuance twice (Morning/ evening)No OS tickets should be remained untouched in a 3+day bucket.All 15day+ ageing OS tickets need to be reviewed and dispositions to be checked, Here RM will own the responsibility to follow and close these tickets.Any request for PDC extension will follow like - L1 to escalate to L2 - manager discussed and then sends for approval after talking to the client.All deals > 1lac - only BM and RM to forward for approval after discussion.No ticket closure on partial payment but temp block can be removed post-ZM approval.BD team will work on the o/s ticket too posts temp block of the catalog.Please Note: Ticket can only be closed post receipt entry of the payment, or if the deal gets converted or the client’s catalog got downloadedHot leads TicketsAll HL Tickets need to be touched within 15mins of issuance. (Morning/Evening)Once we will get proper response from customer below action can be performed basis customer response:Not Interested – The ticket can be closedInterested – Keep the ticket open until final conversion.However, if a client is not responding, the Ticket can be closed post following Non-contactable SOP.Severity is applicable for both ticket typesSeverity 3 is applicable on follow-up miss or delay in follow-up.Severity 2 is applicable on delay in the closure, wrong closure, and incomplete closure.Severity 1 on mis-commitment
