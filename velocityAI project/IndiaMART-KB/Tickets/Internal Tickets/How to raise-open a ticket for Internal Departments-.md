# How to raise/open a ticket for Internal Departments?

The list of Internal Buckets has been bifurcated into below-mentioned categories basis the role of the client in raising these concerns:1. Core Internal Queries (Access & Visibility in servicing is only given to RM and Above)2. Customer Oriented Internal Queries (Access to all)Bifurcation of Internal Queries and auto-tagging logic for Customer-Oriented Internal Queries.Note: The FSF teams’ have already been given access based on the list of users shared by the SPOCs to raise the internal tickets directly.For servicing team, In case a user with Designation RM and above does not have this access to raise (Core Internal Queries) kindly raise the webERP permission request by accessing this link ((https://weberp.intermesh.net/Misc/MyLeftPane.aspx)) ((WebERP >> My ERP >> Permission))For FSF, in case a user with the designation of Sr. Executive and above do not have the access to raise (Core Internal Queries) kindly raise the webERP permission request by accessing this link ((https://weberp.intermesh.net/Misc/MyLeftPane.aspx)) ((WebERP >> My ERP >> Permission))Walk- Through on how to raise a ticket for internal departmentsStep 1:In open ticket screen, select the type of ticket (Core Internal queries / Customer oriented Internal Queries)
