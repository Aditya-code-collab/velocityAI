# SOP for Tester to Handle Absurd Price/Unit Seller Issues

Whenever a seller issues a complaint regarding an absurd unit or price products. The following SOP is to be followed for resolving the issueCase 1: Absurdity is marked when unit is not added as Recommended seller unitExample:For MCAT: TMT BARS, the recommended seller units are Kg,Ton,Quintal & Piece. As ‘Litre’ is not added as a recommended seller unit the product got marked for absurd unitSolution: The CSD team needs to approach the category team. The category owner will check if the desired unit is relevant for the category or not. If the unit is relevant then the category owner can add the desired unit as “Recommended Seller Unit” from the price analytics screenCase 2: Absurdity is marked when a unit does not exist on IM & a new unit creation is required Solution: The CSD team should approach the category owner first. The category owner should do a primary & secondary search for validating the unit relevancy.
