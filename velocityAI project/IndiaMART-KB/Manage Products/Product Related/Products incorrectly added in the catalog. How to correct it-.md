# Products incorrectly added in the catalog. How to correct it?

Identify the product(s) which has been incorrectly added in the catalog.Educate how to "Manage Products" from Seller Panel.If the client requests IndiaMART to make the changes on his behalf then:- For Contact Center: Inform the client to share the product details at "customercare@indiamart.com" where modifications are required.- For Servicing: Inform the client to share the product details via email and raise a self ticket to make the required changes.
