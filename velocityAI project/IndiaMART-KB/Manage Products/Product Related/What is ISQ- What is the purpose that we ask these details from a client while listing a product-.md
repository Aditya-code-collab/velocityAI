# What is ISQ? What is the purpose that we ask these details from a client while listing a product?

ISQ stands for "Industry Specific Questions". IndiaMART database has certain pre-defined fields based on the client's product range/product industry which clients need to fill while filling the listing details.ISQ makes the listing more descriptive which helps the buyers to choose the exact product basis their requirement.The client has a list of ISQs to select from the basis of their product range. Additionally, if the client is unable to find the relevant ISQs for their product range they can share the feedback so that our internal team reviews this feedback regularly for addition and removal or ISQs.ISQs are an essential feature of IndiaMART that bridge the gap between buyers and sellers by providing a clear and structured way to share product-specific details. For sellers, it increases visibility and credibility, while for buyers, it simplifies the decision-making process.Benefits of ISQs for SellersImproved Visibility: Products with complete ISQs rank higher in search results on IndiaMART.Better Lead Quality: ISQs help attract serious buyers who are interested in the specific attributes of a product.Streamlined Communication: By answering common questions upfront, ISQs reduce repetitive queries and save time.Professionalism: A detailed ISQ setup makes the seller's catalog appear more professional and trustworthy.Benefits of ISQs for BuyersInformed Decision-Making: Buyers can evaluate a product’s suitability based on ISQs before reaching out to the seller.Efficient Search: Filters based on ISQs make it easier for buyers to find the exact products they need.Better Experience: ISQs eliminate ambiguity, ensuring buyers receive accurate and relevant information.Configure ISQConfiguring ISQ refers to the process of setting up specific questions for a product category to define its attributes. This involves customizing or selecting predefined ISQs to make product listings more detailed and relevant.Key ISQKey ISQs are the most critical and commonly searched product-specific questions that buyers use to filter and identify the right products. These are essential for improving visibility in search results.Regular ISQRegular ISQs are additional or supplementary questions that provide further details about a product. They are not as critical as Key ISQs but still enhance the overall product listing.
