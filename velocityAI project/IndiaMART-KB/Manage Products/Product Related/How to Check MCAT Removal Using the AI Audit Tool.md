# How to Check MCAT Removal Using the AI Audit Tool

OverviewIn many cases, MCATs (categories) get removed from products, leading to confusion and unnecessary escalations to the CSD Tester or Product teams. To address this, the Product team has introduced the AI Audit Tool, which helps identify the exact reason for MCAT removal.Using this tool allows teams to share first-hand, system-driven information with clients and resolve issues quicklyWhen to Use the AI Audit ToolUse this tool when:A client reports that their MCAT/category has been removedYou need to identify the exact reason for category removalYou want to guide the client with immediate corrective actionsExample CaseGLID: 246159935In this GLID, MCATs were removed from 13 products.(Refer to the relevant screenshots for visual guidance.)Steps to Check MCAT Removal ReasonOpen the Product Catalog for the given GLID.Click on the highlighted option “Use AI to Audit.”A new window will open displaying multiple validation parameters.Look for cross (❌) marks against specific checks.Hover over the cross marks to view the exact reason for MCAT removal.The highlighted reasons clearly explain why the category was removed.(Refer to the relevant screenshots for reference.)Common Reasons for MCAT RemovalAn MCAT may be removed due to one or more of the following mismatches:Product image does not match the product nameProduct details do not match ISQ (Item Specification Questions)Product image, product name, ISQ, and description are inconsistentProduct image differs from the actual product being listedIf any such mismatch is detected, the system automatically removes the MCAT.Corrective ActionsShare the AI Audit findings directly with the client so they can correct the detailsYou may also update the catalog details yourself, if requiredEnsure all corrections are made in one go to avoid delays⚠️ Important Note:Any catalog modification takes 24–48 hours to reflect and get approvedAvoid making repeated changes during this timeWait at least 24 hours before making further modificationsKey Benefits of Using the AI Audit ToolNo need to raise a tester for MCAT-related issuesFaster resolution with reduced turnaround timeAccurate, system-driven insightsBetter client communication with clear reasons and solutionsSummaryThe AI Audit Tool is the preferred first step for checking MCAT removals. It enables quicker diagnosis, eliminates unnecessary escalations, and helps resolve client issues efficiently with transparency and confidence.
