# How to manage product through the Mobile App?

Steps to manage the  ProductStep 1:- Scroll down on Dashboard you will get an option of 'Manage Product' tabStep 2:- Click on 'view all' option, to view all added productsStep 3:- To edit the product, click on the pencil icon.For ImageClick on imageClick on change photo and UploadFor CategoryClick on edit CategoryAdd and edit categories from the option ProvidedUpdate ProductFor Product SpecificationsScroll down the same page and Price, MOQ, Product group & Product Description on the same pageStep 4:- Click on Update products to make the changes
