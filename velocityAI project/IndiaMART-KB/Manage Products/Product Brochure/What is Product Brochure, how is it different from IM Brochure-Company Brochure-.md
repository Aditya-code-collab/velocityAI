# What is Product Brochure, how is it different from IM Brochure/Company Brochure?

A product brochure is a part of the Manage Products section however IndiaMART/ Company brochure is a part of Manage profile section. Mentioned below are the differences between Product and IndiaMART/ Company Brochure: Product Brochure: Which show details, descriptions and specification of a specific product is known as "Product Brochure".IndiaMART/ Company Brochure: It is a system-generated PDF brochure which automatically gets created basis the customer’s catalogue. It is not necessary that IM brochure/Company brochure is always system generated, in case the client wants to display there own personalized IM/Company brochure then they can do the same.Note: In case the personal brochure(IndiaMART/ Corporate) shared by the seller is related to product description and specification, then ideally it should be added as Product PDF.
