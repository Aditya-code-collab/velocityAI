# How to check premium category listing?

Follow the below steps to know how to check the clients listingLog in to 'Seller.indiamart.com'Click on 'Manage Products'Go to 'More Options'Click on 'Product Categories'Click on the category for which you wish to view the listingYou will be redirected to dir.indiamart.com (Listing will be visible along with the Company name and ribbon)
