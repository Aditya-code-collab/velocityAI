# Client wants to re-arrange products/images appearing on Homepage and defined categories

The client can rearrange the product with the top Rank with Maximum score in his catalog only.To understand how these products are displayed need to understand the logic behind the same.We have introduced ABC Mcat Rank Logic in various sections of catalogs.For Home Page (Top Products Section) we have done the new implementation as follows:Priority will be given to the PMCAT Rank From A to B, B To C, then C to DFor example: If the client wants any B-rank product to be displayed on the homepage then it can only be done if he doesn't have A-Rank product. A rank product is given topmost priority.Within the Rank - Product with the maximum product score will be displayed on the topFor example: The client has 4 products with Rank A but has different product score.Product 1- Score is 100Product 2 - Score is 75Product 3 - Score is 80Product 4 - Score is 90The products will be displayed in the following sequence:Product 1Product 4Product 3Product 2Products listing order on their respective User Defined Categories.Ordering of User-Defined Categories (As per the categories and product displayed in the seller panel)For example: The client has 4 products with Rank A but has different product score.Product 1- Score is 100 (Product Category A)Product 2 - Score is 75 (Product Category A)Product 3 - Score is 80 (Product Category B)Product 4 - Score is 100 (Product Category A) - This product is shown above the Product AThe products will be displayed in the following sequence:Product 4Product 1Product 2Product 3
