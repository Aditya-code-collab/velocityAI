# Client not able to find BuyLeads for his/her product. What to do?

To search BuyLeads for the products, educate the client to use the search box on Seller panel. Select ‘BuyLeads’ from the drop-down on the search box and enter the product name. All the BuyLeads with those product specifications will be displayed. The client can then shortlist them basis the recency, order volume, location, etc.If the client is not able to find BuyLead for any specific product then check the basic checkpoints from your endClick here to check our customers' help section for this article.
