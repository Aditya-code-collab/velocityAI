# BuyLead Points/ allocation/ purchase limit in case of defaulter

If the supplier is any kind of defaulter (Email etc) then only 7 India BuyLeads will be allocated per week irrespective of the paid services except Buyer supplier conflict defaulter and PNS.We make the suppliers purchase limit to 0 in such cases until the Buyer-Supplier conflict is resolved.In the case of PNS defaulter, the client will get full BuyLeads points (as per the package availed)
