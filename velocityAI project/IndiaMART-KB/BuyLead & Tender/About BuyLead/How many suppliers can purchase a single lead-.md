# How many suppliers can purchase a single lead?

A single BuyLead can be purchased by up to 5 suppliers.This limit ensures that:The buyer is not overwhelmed with too many calls or messages.The suppliers who consume the lead get a fair opportunity to convert it.The interaction remains relevant and manageable for both sides.Apart from these 5 suppliers, the buyer may also get connected with a few additional sellers through other channels (like AST, PNS, ENQ, or BizFeed), but the BuyLead purchase limit remains fixed at 5.
