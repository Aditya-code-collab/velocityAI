# View latest and consumed tender details from seller panel

Tenders can be consumed by logging in to the Seller panel:(Seller.IM >> BuyLeads >> Latest Tender)Click on "View Tender Information" to consume tender:If the client is unable to consume tender, check hereTo view consumed tender details, click on "Consumed Leads & Tenders"Click on "Consumed Tenders" tab on the top of the screen.Click on Click Here to View Actual Notification to get tender details.
