# Difference between BuyLead and Enquiry

Point of DifferenceBuyLeadEnquiryHow it is raisedA buyer shares a general purchase requirement on the IndiaMART platform.A buyer shows interest in a specific product listed on a supplier’s catalog.Buyer’s awareness of supplierThe buyer does not know which suppliers will receive the requirement.The buyer already knows the supplier and chooses to contact them directly.Interaction patternOne buyer’s need is shared with multiple relevant suppliers.It is a direct one-to-one interaction between the buyer and the selected supplier.VerificationThe requirement is screened and validated by IndiaMART before being shared.Enquiries are raised instantly without any verification process.Supplier AccessOnly suppliers who choose to consume the BuyLead get the buyer’s contact details.The supplier automatically receives the buyer’s contact and inquiry details.Supplier CountA limited number of suppliers (up to 9) can consume the same BuyLead.A buyer can send enquiries to any number of suppliers individually.
