# How to check recent India BuyLeads on Seller panel?

Log in to your Seller Panel through: https://seller.indiamart.comFrom the dashboard menu, go to BuyLeads.Click on the Recent India BuyLeads tab.Here, the client can see the most recent India BuyLeads, along with recommended cities where consuming these leads may yield better results, based on their previous BuyLead usage and response patterns.
