# Benefits of a BuyLead

Suppliers get instant access to new buyers.Details of BuyLeads (i.e. buyer requirement) is visible to suppliers even before consuming it. Hence, suppliers have the choice of selecting and consuming the Buy Leads only if they find it useful.Suppliers get instant notification of most recent and relevant BuyLeads through multiple channels (email, SMS and app notification).Helps supplier in reducing the dependency on existing buyers, thereby, giving suppliers opportunity for expanding their business.
