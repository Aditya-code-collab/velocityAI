# What is BuyLead?

BuyLead is the purchase requirement sent by prospective buyers for products and services, which is filtered by IndiaMART and shared with the suppliers dealing in those products/services.The BuyLead alerts are sent to relevant suppliers via email, SMS and app notification.Suppliers then consume the relevant BuyLead.By consuming a BuyLead, the supplier gets access to the buyer’s contact details.Click here to check our customers' help section for this article.
