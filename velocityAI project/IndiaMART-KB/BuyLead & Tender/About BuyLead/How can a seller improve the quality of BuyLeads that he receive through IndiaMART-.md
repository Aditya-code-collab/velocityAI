# How can a seller improve the quality of BuyLeads that he receive through IndiaMART?

Improving the quality of BuyLeads is a combined result of your platform activity, catalog accuracy, and clear preference settings. By following the practices below, sellers can significantly increase the relevance, usefulness, and conversion potential of their BuyLeads.1. Consume BuyLeads ConsistentlyRegular consumption helps IndiaMART understand what you truly prefer.Check and consume your IMA Weekly BuyLeads daily.Higher consumption directly improves the personalization of future BuyLeads.Avoid missing out on fresh, high-intent leads.2. Refine Your BuyLead PreferencesUse the BuyLead Preference settings to filter out what you do NOT want.Add unwanted cities under Negative City.Add irrelevant categories (MCATs) under Negative MCAT.This “negative marking” ensures the system stops showing leads outside your business interest.3. Share Feedback on Irrelevant BuyLeadsYour feedback trains the recommendation engine.Always mark irrelevant BuyLeads as “Not Relevant.”IndiaMART uses this to refine future matchmaking.The more precise your feedback, the more accurate your lead suggestions become.4. Maintain Accurate Product & MCAT MappingA clean and well-mapped catalog leads to better-quality leads.Map every product to its correct MCAT.Remove outdated or irrelevant products.Keep product details updated and aligned with your actual offerings.Accurate mapping = Better matchmaking = Higher-quality leads.5. Use BuyLead Filters SmartlyFilters help you quickly focus on high-quality opportunities.Use filters for:LocationOrder ValueLead TypeTop CategoriesThis helps you prioritise and interact only with the most relevant leads.6. Stay Active & ResponsiveYour activity positively influences your BuyLead quality and matchmaking signals.Spend 1–2 hours daily responding to calls, enquiries, and leads.Follow up regularly with buyers.Maintain a strong response pattern — it boosts visibility and improves buyer-seller matching.7. Keep Your Catalog UpdatedA well-maintained catalog ensures buyers get accurate information.Add only products that you genuinely deal in.Ensure products are in stock and updated.Remove discontinued items.This prevents irrelevant BuyLeads caused by outdated catalog content.In SummaryTo receive better-quality BuyLeads:✔ Stay active✔ Keep your catalog accurate✔ Use preference settings✔ Provide consistent feedback✔ Consume your leads regularlyBy following these practices, sellers experience a noticeable improvement in relevance, conversion quality, and business impact from IndiaMART BuyLeads.Click here to check our customers' help section for this article.
