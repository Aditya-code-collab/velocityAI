# Supplier not able to purchase BuyLead

Below points need to be checked by Servicing and Customer care team :Check-in history from WebERPwhether the client has been marked as Buyer-Supplier conflict defaulterIf yes - Ask the supplier to resolve the Buyer supplier conflict (BSC) asap. (We make the suppliers purchase limit to 0 in such cases until the BSC is resolved.Please note: Once the BSC defaulter tag is removed it takes 24 working hours for the leads to be allotted again)
