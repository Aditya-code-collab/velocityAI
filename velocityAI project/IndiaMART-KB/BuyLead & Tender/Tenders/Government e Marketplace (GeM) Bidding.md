# Government e Marketplace (GeM) Bidding

Disclaimer: This document is meant for IndiaMART internal use only please do not share any part of this document with the customers.This document can be used as a reference for troubleshooting your client’s problems in the GEM bidding process.The screenshots and steps illustrated in this document are shown as examples only because the GeM Website is managed by the government and the processes as well as the UI/UX keeps changing.The steps shown in this document are subject to change as per the product category and government regulationsPlease go through the previous documents before proceeding with this -Government e Marketplace (GeM) Seller Registration ProcessGovernment e Marketplace (GeM) Product AdditionNote -Only Bids related to the categories in your catalogue will be shown to you.For example – If you want to bid on a Tenders of Mild Steel you will need to add at least one product in the Steel category.Steps to create bids -Search for the Tender you want to bid in using the GEM Publication ID you have got from IndiaMART (it will be of the form GEM/2024/B/4586696)Click ‘Participate’View the bid details and read the details correctlyScroll to the Technical BID section and click ‘Participate’.Now select the product that you want to sell to the buyer -Click on choose this item.Note : Sometimes you will not see any products in this section because either the exact product is not in your catalogue or there is a specification mismatch. In that case you have to add a product or edit the catalogue.Now verify your product details and click ‘Save & Continue’Now the Technical Bid has been saved and it will show in green -Click continue to proceed Now you have to mention your commercials in the bid i.e price. On GEM Bid your unit price should include your GST, Transpiration cost, profit margin etc.Now click on ‘Encrypt & Save’Now the final stage is to upload the documentsNote: The required documents will be different depending on the tender you are bidding onUpload your documents and click on ‘Save’ to continue.Now all that’s left is for you to e-sign your Bid -
