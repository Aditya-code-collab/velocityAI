# Government e Marketplace (GeM) Product Addition

Disclaimer: This document is meant for IndiaMART internal use only please do not share any part of this document with the customers.This document can be used as a reference for troubleshooting your client’s problems in the GEM catalogue creation process.The screenshots and steps illustrated in this document are shown as examples only because the GeM Website is managed by the government and the processes as well as the UI/UX keeps changing.Before starting you can check out all the GEM categories here - https://admin-mkp.gem.gov.in/cat/browse_nodes/categoriesAlternatively, you can search for it on mkp.gem.gov.in/marketSteps to add products to your catalog -Search for your category as shown above and click on ‘Sell in this Category’ or Select ‘Add New Offering’ from the catalog dropdown -Now you will be taken to the category selection screen -Now patiently fill in the details of your product. As shown below -Now you need to provide the catalogue information. Select ‘No’ for Is there any active bid for this catalogue.Provide the authorization info is required in your product category along with the documents.Then proceed to image addition (read the image guidelines carefully)Now your product has been added and it’s waiting for Admin clearanceOnce cleared by the Admin you can bid on those product Tenders on GEM.
