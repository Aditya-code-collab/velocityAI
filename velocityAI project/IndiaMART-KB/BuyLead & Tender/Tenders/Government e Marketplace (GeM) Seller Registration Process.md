# Government e Marketplace (GeM) Seller Registration Process

Disclaimer: This document is meant for IndiaMART internal use only please do not share any part of this document with the customers.This document can be used as a reference for troubleshooting your client’s problems in the GEM registration process.The screenshots and steps illustrated in this document are shown as examples only because the government manages the GeM Website. Thus the Tendering steps as well as the UI/UX keep changing.Sellers have to be registered on the GEM Portal -  https://gem.gov.in/ if they want to bid on GEM Tenders they have consumed on IndiaMART.Video Guide provided by GeM - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=28qTIG-ltLIPre-Requisite for GeM Seller / Service provider Registration and Profile update - https://assets-bg.gem.gov.in/resources/upload/shared_doc/Pre_Requisite-SellerandServiceProviders_GeM_4.0.pdfPoints to note before beginning the registration process -Registration on GeM should be done only by the Key/Authorised Person of the business/organization.Keep all the documents listed in the pre-requisites handy.The registration process is divided into two stages User ID creation and business profile completion.Steps to follow for User Creation -Visit - https://mkp.gem.gov.in/registration/signup#!/sellerSelect the organization type and the documents in your possession -Accept the T&C -On the next screen provide your Business NameChoose either Aadhar or PAN for user verification and enter the OTPVerify your email address -Create your GEM User ID & Password -Now your User ID is created.Steps to follow for Completing Business Profile -Log in with your User ID & password -Start by providing your Business PAN details -Business Details If your organization type is company then your business details will be verified based on your CIN Number. Enter your details as shown below -If your business type is anything other than company then provide your date of incorporation to proceed -Next, provide the additional details as applicable for your business -In case your business qualifies as an MSME provide your Udyam number -In the case of a startup, you will need to fill in the following additional details -Next select your office type & provide your office name then fill in the details as indicated -Now you need to provide your company ITR details -You are almost there! Now enter the bank account details -Provide your Turnover details -To get 100 % business profile completion you will need to complete the optional steps too.Optional 1 : Tax AssessmentOptional 2 : MSEOptional 3 : StartupOptional 4 : Beneficial Ownership CertificateOptional 5 : Logistics - Select your logistics service providerOptional 6 : TAN ValidationAfter that, you should see your Seller Profile like this -
