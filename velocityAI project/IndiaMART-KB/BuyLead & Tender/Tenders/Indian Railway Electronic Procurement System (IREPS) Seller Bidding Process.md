# Indian Railway Electronic Procurement System (IREPS) Seller Bidding Process

Disclaimer: This document is meant for IndiaMART internal use only please do not share any part of this document with the customers.This document can be used as a reference for troubleshooting your client’s problems in the IREPS registration process.The screenshots and steps illustrated in this document are shown as examples only because the government manages the Railway Website. Thus the Tendering steps as well as the UI/UX keep changing.The steps shown in this document are subject to change as per the type of Tender, product category and government regulations.Please go through the previous documents before proceeding with this -Indian Railway Electronic Procurement System (IREPS) Seller Registration ProcessSome of the common symbols/icons used in the IREPS Web application are as follows - Login and search your Tender -Click on Login from the Homage (https://www.ireps.gov.in/)  or Visit - https://www.ireps.gov.in/epsn/jsp/admin/home/logon.jspSimilar to when you registered on the IREPS website insert your Digital Signing Certificate (DSC) USB token in USB drive of your computer, enter your Username and Password (sent by EPS admin through E-Mail) on the Login page, and click on Login button. If a Java Security warning pop-up is shown, please click on the Continue button.This will open the web signer pop-up.Note: It is mandatory to change your password when you are logging in for the first time. Hence reset your password before continuing -After this you will be taken to Bidder Home page.Now from the left navigation plane click on “Search Tenders”. The UI will look something like thisHere enter the details of the Tender you want to Bid on -The Tender Search results will look something like this-You will see different action icons on the right side. Click on the ‘Bid Preparation and Submission’ icon ()Bid Preparation and Submission -After clicking this icon you might get the Responsiveness page. Which are the T&C. Quoting of deviation in respect of any of these clauses shall lead to summary rejection of the Bid.You will have to select the conditions and then click ‘Submit’Now you can see the main bidding screen here you will have to fill the details in each of the tabs before proceeding as illustrated in the following steps -General Tab - This tab displays details like Tender Calling Authority, Tender Title, General Instructions, and Special Conditions. The bidder is required to tick on the check box available at the bottom of the General tab as a mark of acceptance of the conditions displayed on this tab, and also to confirm that he has downloaded, read, and understood the complete tender document, documents attached with the tender, and corrigenda issued, if anyT&C Tab - Here you should read the conditions carefully and accept them.Important: The bidder is required to select one of the payment terms mentioned on the payments page. Eligibility Tab - If confirmation of the bidder is sought on any of the eligibility conditions has been sought by the tendering department, the bidder has to select Yes / No radio button as a mark of compliance. If such confirmation has not been sought, the radio buttons will not be available to the bidder (as is the case in the screenshot shown) - Next you might need to upload the documents. Note: Each uploaded PDF needs to be signed also-Compliance Conditions Tab - If confirmation of the bidder on any of the eligibility conditions has been sought by the tendering department, the bidder has to select Yes / No radio button as a mark of compliance. And you might need to upload supporting documents like the eligibility section.Deviations Tab - Here, the bidder can submit Technical deviations (related to specifications, drawings or any technical requirements) or Commercial deviations (related to any commercial condition of tender) by choosing the suitable radio button against the Deviation Type field.[You can leave this section empty in case you are fully compliant.]Bidders are advised to read the clauses mentioned at the bottom of deviations page carefully before ticking the check box, as quoting deviations against certain conditions may lead to summary rejection of their offer. Performance Tab - Bidders are required to fill in all the required details in the fields provided and click on the Save button. After the details have been saved in, the bidders have to upload copy of Contract document / Purchase order as well as the proof of execution of the contract (Receipt Note or Completion report issued by the consignee)Docs Tab - Tab allows the user to upload miscellaneous documents against the tender. (and the documents need to signed as described before).Note - Bidders should refrain from attaching general documents like Company profile etc. through this tab unless the tender conditions specifically seek these documents. Such general document like company profile etc. should be uploaded only once through the View / Upload documents link in the Documents section in the left navigation block of the Home page as public documents. All such public documents uploaded by the bidder can be seen by the tendering authority. Responsiveness Tab - Follow the steps as shown in the screenshot -Fin. Offer Tab (Financial Offer) - The options will vary depending on the Tender the user is advised to read through these careful and fill in the best quotes. If the tender contains groups of items, the selection panel also contains the group codes. Clicking on any item code expands the rate panel for that item and collapses the rate panels for all other items as shown below: Submit Offer Tab - Here you can see the undertaking and click lists that you as a bidder must follow you have to accept and submit your bid -After this you will see the confirmation screen -
