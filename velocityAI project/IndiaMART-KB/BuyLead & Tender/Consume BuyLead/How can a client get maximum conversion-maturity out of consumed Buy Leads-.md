# How can a client get maximum conversion/maturity out of consumed Buy Leads?

To achieve the highest conversion and maturity from the BuyLeads consumed on IndiaMART, clients should follow a disciplined and proactive approach. The more timely and consistent the engagement, the better the chances of securing business from buyers.1. Contact Buyers ImmediatelyQuick response greatly increases conversion probability.Call the buyer as soon as you consume the lead.Send an email or WhatsApp message introducing yourself and acknowledging the requirement.Fast responders consistently outperform competitors.2. Share Quotations Without DelayBuyers prefer suppliers who provide complete information quickly.Send a clear and detailed quotation immediately based on the buyer’s requirement.Include product specifications, pricing, delivery timelines, and payment terms.3. Maintain Regular Follow-UpsMany deals convert only because the supplier follows up consistently.Schedule follow-ups through calls, email, or WhatsApp.Keep the conversation open-ended to encourage further response.Track all communication using Lead Manager.4. Consume BuyLeads Daily & ActivelyYour consumption pattern influences the quality of future BuyLeads.Consume all allocated BuyLeads daily or at least 2 per day.Avoid letting BuyLeads lapse — fresh leads have the highest maturity potential.Prioritize recent and highly relevant leads.5. Stay Active for 1–2 Hours DailyRegular platform engagement directly improves matchmaking.Spend time daily on:Responding to buyer calls and enquiriesReviewing new BuyLeadsConducting follow-upsUpdating catalogs and product detailsActive users receive better recommendations and improved lead relevance.6. Keep Prices CompetitiveBuyers often compare multiple quotations.Ensure your pricing is competitive and aligned with the market.Highlight offers, bulk pricing, or value-added services if available.7. Leverage Relevance SignalsYour actions help IndiaMART improve your lead recommendations.Consume relevant leads regularly.Provide “Not Relevant” feedback on unsuitable leads.Ensure accurate product-MCAT mapping.This strengthens your lead quality over time.8. Move Faster Than CompetitorsMany suppliers in your location may be active on the same leads.Immediate response and consistent follow-ups significantly increase your success rate.Speed + consistency = higher maturity.In SummaryTo maximize conversions from BuyLeads, clients should:✔ Respond instantly✔ Share quotations quickly✔ Follow up consistently✔ Consume leads daily✔ Stay active on the platform✔ Maintain competitive pricing✔ Use feedback and mapping to improve lead relevanceThese practices help turn buyer inquiries into real business opportunities and ensure maximum value from every BuyLead consumed.
