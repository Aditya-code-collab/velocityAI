We have now started displaying Foreign BuyLead consumption on WebERP.This feature will help you to:Track daily, weekly, and monthly Foreign BuyLead consumption of a sellerCheck their number of active daysAnalyze their behaviour and take actions accordinglyPath To Check Consumed Foreign BuyLeads-Open the desired CompanyClick on ActivityA pop up will appearUnder the daily, weekly or monthly BL tab there is a Foreign optionClick on the radio Button 'Foreign'.
