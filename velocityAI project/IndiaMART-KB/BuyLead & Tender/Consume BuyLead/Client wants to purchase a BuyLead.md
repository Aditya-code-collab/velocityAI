# Client wants to purchase a BuyLead

All the paid subscriptions at IndiaMART have different BuyLead packages wherein the client receives the BuyLead credits every week. Educate the client about the features of different subscriptions as per their requirement or guide the client to check the details on "corporate.indiamart.com"In case the client wants to purchase extra BuyLeads in addition to the weekly credits guide the seller to click on the buylead that he wishes to purchase. Automatically he will be redirected to the packages section which he can purchase.
