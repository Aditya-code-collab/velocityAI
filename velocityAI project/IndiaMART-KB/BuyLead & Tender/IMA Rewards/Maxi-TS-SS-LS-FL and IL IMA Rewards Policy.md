# Maxi/TS/SS/LS/FL and IL IMA Rewards Policy

IMA (IndiaMART Advantage Program) Rewards -Is a program is to strengthen the engagement of our clients. Under the IMA Rewards program, you get back the number of BuyLeads which you consume within the first 4 weeks of hosting.Eligibility - All New Sales* & Service upgrades (Up-sell happening on TS, Maxi, SS, LS, FL, IL)Features:All India BuyLead consumed by a customer in First 4 weeks from the hosting will be rewarded back    into his BuyLead accountThis allocation will be done only once in a service duration on a new service upgradeIt is applicable only when a client is opting for a new MAXI, TS, SS, LS, FL, IL serviceThe maximum number of IMA Rewards that can be earned, is equal to the sum of 4 Week's allocation * Not applicable in case of New MDC Unused India BuyLeads will lapse after one year of their allocation, on the first day of the next monthFor example-- BuyLeads which are allocated on 15 August 2019 will lapse on 1 September 2020.- Additionally, if BuyLeads are allocated more than once a year, they will lapse after one year of the latest date of allocation.For example, if yearly lapsable BuyLeads were allocated on 15 August 2019 and are further allocated on 20 September 2019, then all BuyLeads will lapse on 1 October 2020.
