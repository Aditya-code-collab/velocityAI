# How can client use cashback amount in the IndiaMART subscribed services?

"Cashback" feature is on the MERP's/WebERP's Proposal module.Please note: This is applicable for CSD/ KCD/ Tele-AnnualHow to apply the Cashback?Visit the 'Proposal' tab & click on 'Create New' buttonSelect 'Opportunity' & click on 'Submit' buttonSelect to apply the available cashback from 'Select Cashback' dropdown & proceed further.Applied Cashback will be displayed on the Preview Proposal screen & continue further.
