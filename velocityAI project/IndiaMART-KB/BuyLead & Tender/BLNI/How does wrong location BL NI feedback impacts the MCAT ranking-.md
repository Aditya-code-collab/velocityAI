The City will be marked negative if the following 2 conditions are met: • Count of BL NI(Wrong LP) for the city is more than or equal to 2 in the last 60 days.• Count of BLNI marked from that location within the last two months, or after last purchase date,     whichever is latest is more than equal to 2.2. If Logic 1 met then NI marked City BL’s will be visible in “Relevant BuyLead”.with the least priority. It will be shown in the seller panel only after all the relevant leads are over.     In “Recent Buy Lead” Section. (The negative marked location will be shown on least priority i.e. below the Blue strip)Note:a) If Above logic reversed city will be auto removed from the negative list on the basis of purchase patternb) In WebERP >> IM Prom >> Buy Leads >> Suggested Leads, Leads visible in this section same as “Recent BuyLead” Not as “Relevant BuyLead”.c)  CPL records don’t qualify for negative cities.
