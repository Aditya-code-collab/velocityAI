# Purpose of marking a BuyLead as BLNI/ not interested

Marking a BuyLead as Not Interested (BLNI) plays a crucial role in improving the overall relevance and quality of BuyLeads shown to a seller. It helps IndiaMART understand what the seller does not want, enabling a more personalized experience.Key Objectives of BLNI Marking1. Improve BuyLead RelevancyBLNI feedback helps the system refine future recommendations so sellers receive more relevant, meaningful, and business-fit BuyLeads.2. Customize BuyLead RecommendationsBased on a seller’s BLNI pattern, the platform adjusts and tailors the BuyLeads displayed according to:Preferred product categoriesRelevant locationsMatching buyer intent3. Negative Marking of Categories & LocationsIf a seller repeatedly marks BuyLeads from certain cities, product categories (MCATs), or lead types as Not Interested, the system may classify them as:Negative MCATsNegative LocationsThis ensures similar irrelevant leads are blocked from appearing going forward.4. Better Understanding of Seller PreferencesBLNI helps the recommendation engine learn:What type of leads the seller avoidsWhat product/category matches they rejectWhat locations they do not operate inWhat order sizes or lead types they don’t serveThis leads to smarter matchmaking and better-quality lead curation.In SummaryMarking BuyLeads as BLNI is not just feedback—it is a key input in customizing and optimizing the entire BuyLead experience. It ensures sellers get:More relevant leadsFewer irrelevant inquiriesHigher efficiency in lead consumptionBetter business opportunities
