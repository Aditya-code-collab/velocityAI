# How does BLNI due to Wrong Product impacts the MCAT ranking?

Wrong Product BuyLead Not Interested (BLNI) feedback directly affects the ranking, visibility, and recommendation quality of an MCAT. Below is the complete logic, combining both the old rules and the updated enhancements:1. Conditions When MCAT Gets Downgraded (Deboosted)An MCAT will be downgraded only if both the following conditions are met:The count of Wrong Product BLNI for that MCAT is more than 2 in the last 60 days.Wrong Product BLNI is more than twice the purchase count for that MCAT:(Wrong Product BLNI) > 2 × (Purchases from that MCAT)(NI / Transaction ratio > 200%)Updated Logic Included:If an MCAT receives ≥ 3 Wrong Product BLNI and the NI/Txn ratio exceeds 2, it triggers the deboosting mechanism.2. How MCAT Ranks Are DowngradedOnce the above conditions are met, the MCAT will be downgraded as follows:A-rank → downgraded to B-rankB-rank → downgraded to C-rankD-rank MCAT → removedC-rank MCAT → remains unchangedUpdated D-Rank Rule:To retain D-rank, an MCAT must not have:More than 2 Wrong Product BLNI/QRF in the last 1 month(Earlier limit was 3; now stricter.) (QRF: Query Relevancy Feedback.)If it exceeds this, the D-rank MCAT may be removed.3. MCAT Visibility ImpactIf the downgrade conditions are met:The affected MCAT will experience lower visibility on buyer side pages.Leads from that MCAT will be deprioritized.Higher BLNI leads to deboosting across multiple systems, reducing exposure.4. Negative MCAT Marking (Seller-Level Blocking)An MCAT can also become negative for a seller if:Wrong Product NI / Txn ratio is ≥ 200%A negative MCAT means:The seller stops receiving leads or recommendations from that MCAT.This logic runs nightly and in real-time.5. Impact on Suggested LeadsWrong Product NI is a major component in the scoring system that decides which MCATs are shown in the "More Leads / Suggested Leads" section.A key factor in the score is:0.4 × (Wrong Product BLNI % of MCAT) / (Max BLNI %)MCATs with high Wrong Product NI are deboosted from Suggested Leads.6. Auto-Upgradation RuleMCAT ranks can still auto-upgrade based on consistent purchase patterns, regardless of past BLNI, as per the standard upgrade logic.Please click on the here to understand MCAT ranking definitions.
