# Client is receiving multiple notifications for the same BuyLead

To ensure that you do not miss any business opportunity through BuyLead, we send you the BuyLead notifications through multiple channels such as Emails, SMS, App notification (provided you have enabled BuyLead alerts through these channels). Latest BuyLeads are also displayed prominently on your seller panel.Additionally, if the buyer’s requirement is not fulfilled, then we send out the same BuyLead to relevant sellers multiple times so that they connect with the buyer without delay and close the deal.
