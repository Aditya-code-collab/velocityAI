# Key Reasons For Low BuyLead Notification Delivery

Push notification delivery depends on multiple external factors — device state, OS behaviour, and network conditions. Not all failures are controllable from our side, but we are actively working on improvements.Root causes01Device inactive or offlineThe device may not be connected when a notification is sent. If it stays offline too long, the notification expires before delivery. Common for infrequent app users or those with unstable connectivity.02Battery optimisation restrictionsAndroid devices (Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, etc.) restrict background activity to conserve battery. If the app is killed or blocked from running in the background, notifications are not delivered. This is one of the biggest causes of intermittent failures.03Low priority or delayed system deliveryNotifications not sent with high priority may be delayed by the OS, especially when the device is idle or in battery saver mode. This results in late or missed delivery.04Notification permissions disabledOn Android 13+, users must explicitly grant notification permission. If denied, or if specific notification channels are turned off, the notification may reach the device but never be shown to the user.05Inactive or outdated device tokensAfter app reinstallation or long inactivity, device tokens become stale. Notifications sent to these tokens fail silently. This also occurs when device info is not refreshed regularly.06Network restrictions (VPN / firewall)Some networks block push notification services. The device may appear online, but the notification channel is restricted — leading to inconsistent or failed delivery.Initiatives to improve delivery rateApp version filteringRolled outStops notifications going to devices on older app versions, which are typically inactive. Tested on 50% of users with ~20% improvement in delivery rate. Rolled out to 100% from 28th May. View ticket ↗Dynamic inactive device filteringIn progressThe system will identify inactive devices based on the last token update date and stop sending notifications if the device has not been active for more than 30 days. Referenced in Android App 13.7.7 BuyLead Notifications release mail.Battery optimisation in-app guidancePlannedAn in-app banner will guide users to disable battery optimisation for the IndiaMART app — particularly helpful on Realme, Xiaomi, and similar brands where background restrictions are most aggressive.Support note: When a user reports missing notifications, check against the six causes above. Advise users to keep the app updated, enable notification permissions, and disable battery optimisation for the IndiaMART app on their device.
