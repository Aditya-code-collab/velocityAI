# How can the supplier check the end date of paid services in seller panel/ seller.indiamart.com?

The supplier can view his paid service end date on his seller panel ‘Dashboard’ section. PFB the steps to be followed -Log in to Seller panelGo to the Dashboard sectionScroll down to ‘Active Services’Under ‘My Services’  supplier can view the end date of his service.Screenshot attached of the seller panel for better understanding -
