# Manual Addition of "Do Not Prefer" City Via Seller panel/ How to provide location feedback through seller panel?

We have now provided the feature to add "Do Not Prefer" city.Now a seller can add any city by himself in the do not prefer city by searching the city name:Steps to follow -Step 1: Log in to Seller.Indiamart.comStep 2: Go to Settings & click on Location Preference TabStep 3: Enter the name of the city & the list will auto-populate. Select the desired city & click on the "Exclude" button.
