# When the TrustSEAL service is made live?

Check that when the Trust seal W/O was issued:If WO is in W.I.P. status:- If <21 days: Inform that our standard TAT is 21 days.- If >21 days: Raise an internal ticket in Trust Seal bucket to get the required changes done.If WO is in Completed status:- Inform the Trust Seal live dateIf WO is in Cancelled status:- Share the reason for cancellation, If client is not agreeing on the reason, raise a ticket for servicing team to arrange a call back.@Servicing Team: Arrange call and inform the detailed reason for cancellation.
