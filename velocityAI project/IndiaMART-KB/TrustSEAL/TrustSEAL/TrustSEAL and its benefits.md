# TrustSEAL and its benefits

What is TrustSEAL?TrustSEAL is IndiaMART’s official verification service that certifies a supplier as genuine, credible, and trustworthy. It strengthens a supplier’s credibility by validating their business details through a structured verification process.TrustSEAL is:A paid add-on for MDC customers, andComplimentary for higher-level services such as Maximiser, Star Supplier, Leading Supplier, and above.How TrustSEAL Verification WorksThe TrustSEAL badge is issued only after completing IndiaMART’s multi-step verification, which includes:Document Verification: Proof of business existence, legal status, statutory registrations, affiliations, and certifications.GST Verification: All submitted documents are GST-verified; the GST status must be active.Bank Verification: Confirms that the bank account holder matches the registered business name.VKYC (Video KYC): 100% mandatory check to validate location, stock, invoices, and company details.Once approved, a supplier is officially marked as a TrustSEAL Verified Supplier on IndiaMART.Benefits of TrustSEALFor SuppliersEnhanced Business Credibility: Demonstrates that the supplier is verified, increasing buyer trust and improving conversions.Higher Visibility Across IndiaMART:TrustSEAL LogoTrustSEAL Stamp on listingsTrustSEAL CertificateTrustSEAL Verification ReportThese appear across major touchpoints such as the catalog homepage, Company Page, PDP, and search results.Better Buyer Engagement: Verified suppliers often receive more views, higher-quality enquiries, and improved listing positions.BuyLeads Advantage:TrustSEAL Catalog clients receive 20 BuyLeads weekly (auto-credited every Sunday at 12:01 AM; unused leads lapse on Saturday at 11:59 PM).Daily Bonus: 2 additional BuyLeads per day, expiring the same day.Access to IndiaMART Advantage (IMA) Programs for added business support.Dedicated Account Manager & Lead Manager tools for seamless lead handling.ECOM-Specific Benefits (TrustSEAL ECOM)Enhanced visibility in specific MCAT + city combinations.Exclusive ECOM catalogue benefits.Complimentary All-India Visibility.A-Rank positioning for paid sellers in selected categories.For BuyersGreater Confidence: Buyers can trust that the supplier’s identity, documents, GST status, bank details, and business operations are verified.Authenticity Assurance: Reduces the risk of interacting with unverified or inconsistent sellers.More Reliable Transactions: TrustSEAL helps buyers make informed decisions—especially helpful for advance-payment scenarios.Transparency: Provides a detailed view of the supplier’s verified credentials, enhancing trust in the purchase process.Click here to check our customers' help section for this article.
