# TrustSEAL Verification Process

Current Process:Work order received for TrustSEAL, GST status of case checked, if active case scheduled for data entryAfter data entry case scheduled for Address Verification (physical)After successful verification, the case is scheduled for TrustSEAL Certificate Printing and CourierTrust Score Generation Scores will be given based on E-Verification, Seller’s profile on IM and information available on Online SourceCustomer Connect:After Score is calculated, then TS team connect with the customer to take an appointment for Video KYCIf Score’s threshold does not met, case is not assigned for data entryIf Score’s threshold is met, case is assigned for data entryVideo KYCCustomers will be asked for Video KYC through a web conference link. This is assisted Video KYC, IndiaMART representatives talk of web meeting and have virtual visits of premisesThe case will be processed for Certificate Printing and Dispatch after 4th step i.e  Trust Seal Approval/RejectionMandatory Parameters for Trust SealGST should be activeLess than 3 Buyer-Supplier Conflict Tickets in Past 1 yearExpectationsThe team should inform the steps of verification at the time of saleThe team should do the expectation setting of the customer regarding video KYCThe team should inform the benefits of the new verification process
