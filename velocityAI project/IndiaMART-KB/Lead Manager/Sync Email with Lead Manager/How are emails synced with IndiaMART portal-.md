# How are emails synced with IndiaMART portal?

This feature provides a unique e-mail id to every Enquiry/ BuyLead email sent by IndiaMART to its premium users. This unique email id is present in the tag of the Enquiry/ BuyLead emailAny reply sent to this email id will be delivered to the buyerIf the buyer replies on the same unique email, the reply will be delivered to youAll these replies will also be synced and visible across your IndiaMART account on desktop as well as IndiaMART app
