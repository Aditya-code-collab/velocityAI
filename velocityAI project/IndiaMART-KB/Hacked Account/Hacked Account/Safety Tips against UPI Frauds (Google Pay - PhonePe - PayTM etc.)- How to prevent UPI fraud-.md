# Safety Tips against UPI Frauds (Google Pay / PhonePe / PayTM etc.)/ How to prevent UPI fraud?

Nowadays frauds by using different UPI Payment methods like PayTM, PhonePe, Google Pay are increasing. It's our responsibility to educate all our suppliers on what all precautions they need to take while getting payment from Buyers using UPI.How these frauds are happening?Fraudsters will send payment request link instead of paymentThey will ask to scan a QR CodeThey might ask you the share your screen using  mobile screen sharing appsWhat are the precautions our suppliers must take to avoid being cheated by such fraudsters?Entering PIN  is not required to get payment from any Buyer using UPINever scan any QR Code to get the payment from any BuyerDo not install any screen sharing apps like Teamviewer, Anydesk or QSUPI Pin is entered only when you make a payment and NOT when you receive money/payment from anyoneNever click on any link available in SMS sent by anyone who want to pay youWatch out for words like Send, Requested by, Pay, etc. They all indicate that money is about to be debited from the seller's accountAlways communicate on an official email address if a buyer is portraying as government servant/official/representativePlease Note: Best way to get money instantly from a buyer is to get money via Pay with IndiaMART payment facilitation
