# Education – Fraudsters Posing as Defence personnel

Educate users that their safety is just as much their responsibility as it is ours.Fraudsters are constantly using new ways to cheat users hence often they pose as Defense personnel to gain trust. These fraudsters may act as a seller or buyer to defraud users.Fraudsters posing as defence personnel and posting fake products as a sellerThese fraudsters will post fake products with tempting prices They will share fake defense ID proofs to gain buyer’s trustAfter gaining buyer’s trust, they will ask for advance payment for the product as token amount, airport charges, etc. After buyer have paid the amount, they will stop responding to buyer’s calls and messagesFraudsters posing as defense personnel and contacting sellers as a BuyerThese fraudsters will contact sellers as buyers and agree to supplier’s asking priceThey will urge to make advance payment for the product They will make a payment of small amount through UPI apps to gain supplier’s trust and act as a legitimate buyer After sending the small amount, they may send supplier QR code for a bigger amount Scanning these QR codes will deduct money from supplier’s account
