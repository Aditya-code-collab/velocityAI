# Weekly PNS defaulter

The Weekly PNS Defaulter process identifies PAID PNS suppliers who are consistently non-responsive to buyer calls. This is a weekly evaluation (Sunday to Saturday) that checks call failures, response behavior, and callback activity.1) 1st Time InformationCriteria:Supplier hosted for more than 1 month>10 missed PNS calls in the last 30 daysFailure % between 30% – 40%2) Pre-WarningCriteria:Supplier hosted for more than 1 month>5 missed PNS calls in the last 23 daysFailure % between 40% – 100%3) WarningCriteria:Supplier already in Pre-Warning stage>50% failure in the last 30 daysAt least 1 call in the last 7 days4) PNS Defaulter Marking(a) New DefaulterSupplier who received a Warning last week and meets the following:>50% failed PNS calls in the last 7 days≥1 call received in the last 7 days(b) Repeat DefaulterSupplier who was a defaulter last week and meets the following:>40% failed PNS calls in the last 7 days≥1 call received in the last 7 daysImportant Notes (Updated with New Logic)Callback Inclusion (NEW)If the supplier:Misses a PNS callCalls back the same buyer (same GLID) on the same day→ The failed call will NOT be counted in defaulter marking.If there are multiple missed calls:Only the failed call with a timestamp earlier than the callback is excluded.Callbacks from all app screens (iOS + Android) are considered.IMA Check Removed (NEW)Earlier, suppliers with high IMA usage were exempted in some cases.IMA activity is no longer considered in PNS defaulter marking or removal.General RulesCalls (success + fail) of less than 10 seconds are excluded.Success % is calculated on unique caller basis.Suppliers in CMR / Outstanding / Bounce status are not marked defaulters.Cases where IMA >75% were earlier excluded, but this condition is now retired.Only buyer-initiated callback patterns matter;
