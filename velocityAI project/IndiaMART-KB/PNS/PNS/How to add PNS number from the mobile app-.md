# How to add PNS number from the mobile app?

Steps for Android phone-Scroll down on Dashboard, Click on Call Response Tab (On dashboard he can view his response % of PNS Calls)It will direct you to the page where you can check the numbers which are linked with PNSCustomer can select office and non office according to his convenience & click on save changes.Click on Plus Sign on right bottom to add more numbers After adding the number (Mobile, Landline, Toll free) Click on save button.
