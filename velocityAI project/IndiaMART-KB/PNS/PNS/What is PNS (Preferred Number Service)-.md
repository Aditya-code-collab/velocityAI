# What is PNS (Preferred Number Service)?

It is a unique virtual number given to the clients by IndiaMART.When a buyer calls on this number, all the numbers of the seller registered with IndiaMART ring simultaneously.This ensures that the seller does not miss any business call.The seller can view the PNS details from his seller panel:Seller panel >> Settings >> PNS SettingsClick here to check our customers' help section for this article.
