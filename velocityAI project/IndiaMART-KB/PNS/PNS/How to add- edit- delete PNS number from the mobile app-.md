# How to add/ edit/ delete PNS number from the mobile app?

Android : Add number in PNSPath:Login Mobile app >> Settings >> Preferred Number Settings (PNS) >> Click on the + Icon >> Add new number >> Click on Save ORScroll down on Dashboard, Click on Call Response Tab (On dashboard he can view his response % of PNS Calls)It will direct you to the page where you can check the numbers which are linked with PNSCustomer can select office and non office according to his convenience & click on save changes.Click on Plus Sign on right bottom to add more numbers After adding the number (Mobile, Landline, Toll free) Click on save button.Please Note: Newly added number will automatically get mapped to PNS. User can map a maximum of 5 numbers to PNSAndroid : Edit number in PNSPath:Login Mobile app >> Settings >> Preferred Number Settings (PNS) >> Click on the number in which you want to make changes >> Click on SaveAndroid : Delete number in PNSCurrently, we do not provide this facility through a mobile app.Alternative solution:The client can delete the number through seller panel or can disable the office hour and non-office hour setting. This way the client will not receive PNS call on that number.Kindly click here if you wish to know how to delete number through seller panel.IOSCurrently, we do not provide these changes in IOS app.We only provide the option of office and office hours enabling and disabling setting.Point to be notedBy selecting both these icons, the seller will get a call on the office as well as on non-office hoursIf both buttons are disabled, the seller will not get calls on that numberThe client can also select one option as per his convenienceWhen seller will select office (9 AM – 7 PM) or non-office hour timing  (9 PM – 7 AM), the number will automatically get mapped to PNS
