# Hosting Related

NACH Related:Q. Is catalog feedback mail required from the client even if the client shares the verbatim on call?A. Yes, feedback mail must be received at the production endAnd guide him to come online and get the genuine feedback from the client.Q. If the Client shared feedback with us but due to tech issue feedback not showing?A. Ask for feedback screenshot/ mail from the registered mail id the client and attach it in STS without fail.'Customer Feedback Mail'Q. Can the catalog be hosted after making the required changes suggested by the client?A. 1.) Do the required changes as suggested by the client/ SOP.2.) Update the client on changes & make the client to come online, get it checked & take final confirmation to host the catalog.Q. Can catalog feedback be taken first & then complete the catalog designing work?A. 1.) No, feedback must not be taken before completing the entire work on the catalog.2.) First, design the catalog & make modifications suggested by the client.3.) Then ask for client's genuine feedback.Note: Taking feedback prior to the complete designing work must not be practice.Q. Client having any technical issue/ product concern, can the catalog be hosted?A. No, proper resolution must be given to the client for his concerns prior to the catalog hosting.(Get it resolve by Sunaina Tikkoo/ Harshit Goel)Q. Can products be deleted/ modified in the catalog without client's confirmation? A. There's a restriction on Employee Login for product deletion on the Product Section of Seller.IM.The employees will not be able to delete products from the catalog.Q. Can call be made on the alternate number for hosting confirmationA. No, Hosting confirmation on primary number from RPC must be taken.If client asks to call on secondary number, then client prefeered person can give confirmation.Q.  What if the client wants to host the catalog on specific date/ change the NACH date?A. 1.) First, guide the client on IM & bank process.2.) Host the catalog only after clearly updating the client & taking the confirmation from the client.3.) Inform NACH Date Can't be Changed post hosting & ensure him that the NACH payment must not be bounced.If Client is rigid- Ask for the most nearby convenient NACH deduction date & ensure that he do not bounce the payment in future.If the client asks for more than 3 days delay, then straight away deny for the same."Q. If the client is not answering the NACH call, can the catalog be hosted?A. No, the catalog has to be hosted only after providing the complete information to client.If client is not reachable, an explicit discussion on feedback/ hosting & reply should have been awaited.Q. When does a catalog goes live?A. Ideally, the catalog gets Live within 7 days. But the Catalog Live time may get delayed due to various factors like:Time required for material collectionTime required to activate NACHClient availabilityQ. If on the last/ hosting call, client requested for product addition or any other modification in the catalogA. Add the products/ complete the modification & then proceed for the hostingQ. The client is not ready to pay via NACH. Do we have any other option?A. No, if the client has taken NACH service then the payment will be collected through Nach auto-debit payment mode only.Q. Why the current account is mandatory?A. Savings account is not allowed in NACH process.As per IndiaMART policy, it is mandatory for the supplier to have the current account.Q. Can the catalog be hold for some time in between?A. No.In monthly as well as annual package, subscription can NOT be on hold for sometime.Q. What is NACH?A. NACH is an Automatic payment deduction from the client's bank account Q. On which date, the catalog would be hosted?A. The auto-selected NACH deduction date will be the nearest date from hosting date & in the multiple of 5 i.e. 5th, 10th,15th, 20th, 25th and 30th of a month Q. Why Nach date is always in multiple of 5?A. It is as per bank ruleQ. What if i want to change my NACH date?A. 1.) Inform the client on auto-generated bank date system which is currently visible on the system.2.) Host the catalog only after clearly updating the NACH date to the client & taking the confirmation from the client.3.) Inform NACH Date Can't be Changed post hosting & ensure him that the NACH payment must not be bounced.Q.  Will we receive a message about auto debit 2-4 days before?A. Yes, auto-debit message will be received before NACH date.Also, there will be a unique date for NACH & it is advisable to set a reminder to keep sufficient balance in the account.Q. If the NACH gets bounced,can the service be activated again,how long does it take?A. After 2 bounce, the catalog gets download.And it takes time to activate it again.Request not to bounce the NACH as the client needs to pay bounce charges around Rs 400-500 & hit CIBIL score.Q. Client shares feedback during the call that "I heard that IM deducted amount after completing 6 months also" on monthly package?A. Yes, amount will deduct unless & untill receive any request from the clientQ. What if I want to Change NACH bank?A. Escalate to the sales team and open a ticketClose the ticket & host the catalog only after the change of bank.Q. Client says: Earlier it is 30 days payment cycle but why it's 25 days cycle now?A. This is as per the policy and infact earlier high amount of set-up charges was taken from the clients which has been reduced now.
