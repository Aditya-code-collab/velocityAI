# FAQ-Pay with IndiaMART

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)Discontinuation of Pay with IndiaMART ServiceQ. What is Pay with IndiaMART?A. Pay with IndiaMART is a service that helps sellers to get payments through multiple modes - credit card, debit card, UPI, net banking, etc. It offers buyers protection against any fraud. Q. Can I use Pay with IndiaMART until April 30th 2024 ?A. Existing paid sellers, where Pay with IndiaMART is enabled,  can use it just like before until April 30. After that, we will be discontinue the service.However, starting from 1st May, the service will no longer be available. For newly hosted sellers, we are stopping this service from Feb 21 onwards & they will have no access to Pay with IndiaMART service. Q. Seller has already shared a payment link with his buyers. What will happen to the payments on the link?A. We will accept payments on the link till 30th April. After that, we will disable the payment links and no further payments can be made.Q.Since I paid for the 'Pay with IndiaMART' service, will I receive a partial refund or compensation?A.We understand your concern regarding discontinuing the 'Pay with IndiaMART' service. Here's what you need to know:No Refunds: The 'Pay with IndiaMART' feature is a part of the broader range of services offered by IndiaMART. As this is an inclusive service and not a separately charged feature, we are not providing refunds. This approach aligns with the terms of service agreed upon during your subscription.Continued Service Value: It's important to note that all our sellers utilize various other valuable services provided by IndiaMART. We continuously work to enhance these services to ensure your business continues to thrive on our platform.Support and Assistance: Our team remains committed to assisting you in leveraging other tools and services available on IndiaMART. We encourage you to explore these options to maximise your business potential.We appreciate your understanding and are here to support you in making the most of the numerous other benefits that IndiaMART offers to its sellers.Q. Will you restart this service in the future?A. We don’t foresee starting this service again. We're always looking for ways to help your business. We'll keep you updated on any new/similar services we introduce.Q. Will this affect seller enquiries and buy leads?A. This change only impacts Pay with IndiaMART and does not affect any other services of IndiaMART. Q. Will Buyer Protection Program be applicable for payments already received?A. All payments received till 30th April, 2024, will continue to be covered under Buyer Protection Program subject to current terms and conditions. Q. How will IndiaMART use my account details which I shared for PWIM payments?A. Account details provided across IndiaMART are not specifically for PWIM payments. They are for your profile enrichment. You can continue to view and edit all your account details under the profile section. Q. How can I collect payment now?A. You can continue to accept payment directly to the Bank account of your choice by existing payment options like UPI, net banking, etc.Q. Is there any way IndiamART to protect my payment to its sellers? (or) Is it safe to pay sellers directly?A. IndiaMART is a matchmaking platform that connects buyers and sellers. We provide information for you to identify the seller performance and ability. We recommend you to verify the seller credentials - based on your experience too. We request the buyers to verify the details of the sellers - by checking the tags on the seller profile  - Leading Supplier, Star Supplier, TrustSeal Verified and Verified Plus supplier before making the payment. We also recommend the buyers to check the ratings & reviews of the seller before making any payment. Q. How will IndiaMART ensure that the buyer is authentic and will clear payments?A. IndiaMART is a matchmaking platform that connects buyers and sellers. We request the buyers to communicate through IndiaMART platforms and also check the buyer status like GST Verification and Buyer History. It is prudent not to share any personal information or unnecessary information. You can also employ other precautions like - getting payment confirmation before initiating the delivery or use reliable logistics providers for the delivery. We recommend you to use your experience to verify the authenticity of the buyers. Q. What Dispositions are to be selected while closing a ticket received against discontinuation of Pay With IM Services?A. Disposition selection should be as below-Category-Billing & PaymentAction Taken- PWIM discontinuationReason Code- Education given or RetainedEducation Given—Choose this reason code for all scenarios in which the client has been educated about these changes.Retained—The client raised a deactivation request due to Pay With IM changes, and CC successfully retained the client.
