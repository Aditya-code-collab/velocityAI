# MERP: How to view service details of the company?

Follow the below steps to view the service details of the company:Login to MERPIt will redirect you to 'Homescreen'Click on 'My Companies'Open the desired company for which you wish to view the details.A company dashboard will appear as belowClick on DetailsClick on Service to view the details(Here you will view the details of the enlists services purchased by the client)
