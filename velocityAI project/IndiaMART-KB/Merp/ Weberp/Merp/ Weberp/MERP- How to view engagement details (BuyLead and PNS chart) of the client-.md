# MERP: How to view engagement details (BuyLead and PNS chart) of the client?

Follow the below steps to view the ticket details of the company:Login to MERPIt will redirect you to 'Homescreen'Click on 'My Companies'Open the desired company for which you wish to view the details.A company dashboard will appear as belowClick on 'Details'Click on 'Engage' to view the details(Detailed report on Buy Leads history as well as PNS success rates in 3 bifurcated tabs)
