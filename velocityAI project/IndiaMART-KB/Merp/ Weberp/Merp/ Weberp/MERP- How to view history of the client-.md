# MERP: How to view history of the client?

Follow the below steps to view the history of the company:Login to MERPIt will redirect you to 'Homescreen'Click on 'My Companies'Open the desired company for which you wish to view the details.A company dashboard will appear as belowClick on 'Details'Click on 'History' to view the details(STS history of a Gl ID with details of meetings & calls in 4 bifurcated tabs)
