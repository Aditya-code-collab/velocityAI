# MERP In house C2C FAQ

Q: I have called many suppliers but none of the records is captured in the reports? A: Please ensure that you have installed the new MERP app through APK and allowed all permissions Q: I have talked to the supplier but the same is not visible in call record or history? A: Please ensure that you are using the latest MERP app downloaded through APK and not from Google Play store. Also, allow all permissions before proceeding Link: https://merp.indiamart.com/c2c/nsdmerp.apk Sometimes few calls take a little more time to get updated in the database due to network issue, but no record is missed. Please check for such records in sometime after making few more calls. Q: I am unable to see C2C call option & other options in the MERP App? A: It can be due to poor internet speed, please check your network connection and try again. Q: I have received an Incoming call from supplier, but it is not captured? A: Incoming Call record is only captured if customer's number (Caller number) is registered with IndiaMART (Primary/Secondary number) and STS must be assigned to you. Q: I have made an Outgoing call to the supplier, but it is not captured? A: Outgoing Call record is captured if the customer's number is dialled using the MERP app only. Q: My Balance is getting deducted while making C2C calls? A: Calls to the customer are being made through your number, hence this deduction. The recommendation here is to switch to a number with an unlimited calling plan. My problem is still not resolved: Please use the MERP feedback option and share your concern with us.Path: MERP APP >>Menu>> Feedback
