# MERP: How to view the ticket details of the company?

Follow the below steps to view the ticket details of the company:Login to MERPIt will redirect you to 'Homescreen'Click on 'My Companies'Open the desired company for which you wish to view the details.A company dashboard will appear as belowClick on 'Details'Click on 'Tickets' to view the details(Here you will view the details of the enlists tickets issued against the GL ID)
