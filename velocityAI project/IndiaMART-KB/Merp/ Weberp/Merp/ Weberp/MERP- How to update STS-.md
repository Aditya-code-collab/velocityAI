# MERP: How to update STS?

Follow the below steps to update the sts details:Login to MERPIt will redirect you to 'Homescreen'Click on 'My Companies'Open the desired company for which you wish to view the details.A company dashboard will appear as belowClick on 'Actions'Click on 'STS'Fill the detailsClick on 'Update'Note: It is mandatory to update STS after call/meeting
