# TS/Maximiser clients are listed below the MDC client

An MDC seller could be visible above TS in any of the following cases -1. Better Product EnrichmentShowing sellers with Product Enrichment on top gives us an advantage in SEO, further bringing more traffic and more Buyers on the Page. Hence we show highly enriched MDC sellers on Top compared to Low enriched TS Sellers. Suggestion to client: Advise the seller to enrich his Products with all the details to be visible on Top. 2. Better MCAT RankWe calculate Mcat rank based on BL Consumption. Sellers who consume Good leads in any Mcat becomes A rank sellers, while sellers who do not consume leads become C Rank sellers. Basis this, for the benefit of Buyer we show A Rank MDC Sellers on Top compared to that of C Rank TS Sellers. Suggestion to client: Consume more Leads in Mcats to have better listing order. 3. Deduced Location PreferenceSellers with DLP Global/Foriegn/India are more relevant and responsive on All india page hence they are shown on top, compared to Local or Hyperlocal Sellers.Also, Local Sellers complain that they are getting irrelevant leads from All India, when we show them on Top.Basis this, we show All India / Foreign / India MDC Sellers on Top, compared to Local /HyperLocal TS Sellers.Suggestion to Client: If he wants to deal in All India and be on Top of All India Page,he should consume more leads related to All India, so that we can show him on Top.
