# Android OTP Not Showing in Inbox (Google Messages)

SummarySome Near-Stock and Stock Android devices (e.g., OnePlus, Nothing, Motorola, Nokia, Pixel, etc.) using the Google Messages app have recently received an update that automatically filters certain messages—including OTPs—into the Spam & blocked folder.Even when OTPs are successfully delivered from IndiaMART’s end, the user may not see them because they are being marked as spam or blocked by the app.ErrorsUser reports “Not receiving OTP”.From IndiaMART backend, OTP status shows Delivered.User is using Google Messages app.Clearing storage, deleting old messages, or restarting the device does not fix the issue.Root CauseDue to the latest Google Messages update, the app may incorrectly categorize OTP messages as:Spam, orBlocked messagesThis prevents the user from seeing the OTP even if it is delivered successfully.Resolution Steps (Guide the Client Step by Step)Ask the client to follow the steps below.(You can paste screenshots for each step.)Step 1: Open Google MessagesAsk the user to open the Google Messages app on their phone.Step 2: Click on the Options IconOn the top-right corner of the Messages screen (right side of the search bar), they will see an options icon (three dots or profile/settings icon depending on the version).Tap on it.Step 3: Open “Spam & blocked”After tapping the icon, a menu will appear.Select “Spam & blocked”.This folder contains all messages flagged by the app.Step 4: Find the IndiaMART OTP MessageInside the Spam & blocked folder, ask the user to:Locate their IndiaMART OTP message.Step 5: Mark as Not Spam / UnblockOnce they open the IndiaMART message:If the message is marked as Spam, they will see the option “Not spam” → tap it.If the sender/message is Blocked, they will see the option “Unblock” → tap it.After removing it from spam or blocked, future OTPs will start appearing normally in their main Messages inbox.Final ConfirmationAsk the user to try requesting the OTP again.It should now be delivered and visible in the main inbox without issues.
