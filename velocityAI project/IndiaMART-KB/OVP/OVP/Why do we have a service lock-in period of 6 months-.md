# Why do we have a service lock-in period of 6 months?

This is required to get settled in online promotion and get good business inquiries which can then be converted into business leads with ease.During this time period supplier should try to maximize his engagement with Buyers through follow up on leads, enquiries and direct calls. Every business requires some time to get recognized by buyers, be it physical market or online business. Thus, supplier expectation setting should be done accordingly.
