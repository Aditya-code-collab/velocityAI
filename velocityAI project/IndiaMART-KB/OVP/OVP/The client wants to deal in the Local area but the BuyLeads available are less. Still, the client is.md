# The client wants to deal in the Local area but the BuyLeads available are less. Still, the client is ready to be onboarded. What to do in such cases?

If the BuyLeads in client's servicing area is less, we shall not provide him paid subscription as this will lead to dissatisfaction in future and might lead to churn. Client shall continue with our free subscription and enrich his profile to receive enquiries and enhance his visibility. He shall be ensured for complete support during his free subscription.
