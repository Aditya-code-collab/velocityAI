# Displaying Enquiries Instantly on Lead Managers & Messages - 2nd July'19

We created a service (called 'finishenquiry') which needs to be called from all the different properties. The purpose of this service is to inform that user has filled all the details and isqs while filling the enquiry form. Once this information is received, email, sms & notification are triggered to the supplier and enriched enquiry is displayed in the lead manager/messages.Previously, when this information was not available, we used to wait for 5-7 mins with an assumption that the buyer might has filled this information. There was a cron which runs every 5 minutes to send these details to the supplier. This cron was also used to push inform in the lead manager table leading to some delay of 5-7 mins.Please Note:Rejected enquiries (enquiries with banned content, etc) were not being displayed in the lead manager. We started with just displaying the enquiries which are approved to both buyer and sellers in lead manager & messages. This process has been enhanced and now rejected enquiries are also shown to the buyers in their message center (but not to the seller)
