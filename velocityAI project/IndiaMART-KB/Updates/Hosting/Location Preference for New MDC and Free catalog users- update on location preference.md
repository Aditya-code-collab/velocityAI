# Location Preference for New MDC and Free catalog users/ update on location preference

We have observed that broad location preference set at the time of paid seller onboarding was leading to lot of irrelevancy and a poor buylead experience, thereby leading to high BL NI and customer churn.To Solve this problem, it has been proposed to have just 2 Location preference options: India and Foreign.So, from now on - All new MDC (Mini Dynamic Catalog) hostings will have India as location preference. Clients will receive BuyLeads from his/her home City(+250 kms)  and his/her preferred cities based on BL Consumption, which can be across India.For Example:If I am a new seller from Noida, hosted on 1st December'19, then I will recieve BuyLeads from My City (+250 Kms) i.e. (Noida + 250 Kms) and My Preferred Cities for eg. Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, based on my BL Consumption.What this means?All new customers will have default location preference as India.In the absence of preferred cities, customers will receive BuyLeads from Local Area Only (Customers' City + 250 Km).If customers want to consume BuyLeads from outside local area, they can do so by using Location Filters (State/India/Foreign) on BL Section of Seller Portal and Android App to find BuyLeads.As the customers consume leads of cities outside their local area, these cities will be added to their Preferred cities list and they will start receiving BuyLeads from these Preferred cities as well.This is similar to what is currently running for our existing paid clients.
