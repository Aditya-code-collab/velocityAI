# Introducing New Custtype - ExportTS

To provide better visibility to our IVE sellers on DIR (as they are High Paying sellers), we have introduced a new CustType - ExportTSThis is allocated as complimentary to suppliers having IVE packages hence it will be visible to all eligible IVE clients.Features:Export TS will have custtype weight same as that of normal TS.Only Foreign BL's will be allocated  [according to the IVE Package chosen]No Indian BL's to be allocated to the clients having Export TSAllocation Logic:Custtype allocation will be as follows:For TS and the above clients, we haven't changed the CustType.For other clients, we have upgraded the CustType to ExportTS.Additional Logics to be noted:The rest of the Export TS framework will be similar to normal TS i.eAll the IVE sellers (now having ExportTS) should have visibility similar to TrustSeal on DIR.If a client is downloaded or his service expires, Export TS should automatically get removed.If a client starts New Export services (IVE) then Export TS should be automatically allocated, as per the conditions mentioned above.TS Certification will be issued to only those clients who have purchased IVE package.DIR Visibility - ImpactVisibility on DIR has been improved due to the introduction of ExportTS to IVE clients.
