# Change in TDS Rate

The TDS rate applicable on payment made for services provided by IndiaMART has changed to 1.5% effective 14 May 2020 as per the notification issued by CBDT (Central Board of Direct Taxes)The earlier rate was 2%. The new rate would hence be applicable to all payments being done by suppliers for availing IndiaMART services from today. This is applicable to all balance payments done from today (14th May 2020) onwards for deals done prior to 14th May 2020 as well.
