# Enabling MDC monthly E Nach registration via Savings and Current Accounts

We have enabled MDC Monthly E-Nach process registration with both Current and Savings Account on both Desktop site and Mobile Site.Earlier, the client could only register their E Nach through current accounts only but now we are also providing users to register e Nach via savings account.
