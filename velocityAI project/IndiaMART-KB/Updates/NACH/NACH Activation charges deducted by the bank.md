# NACH Activation charges deducted by the bank

Amount deducted from the client's account (NACH Processing Bank Account) by the bank on behalf of the Indiamart (Approx deduction between 100 - 999).Please Note: It is a one-time deduction by the bank, which is a NACH Activation Charges and was deducted by the bank once the NACH has been processed/approved by the bank for monthly deduction of the amount for which client has paid to Indiamart.Sample Image
