# Rights for NACH Stop (5/02/2019)

Closure Rights for NACH stop in Monthly Mode has been given to BM/CCM and above.The BM/CCM now can stop NACH (Monthly) for a paid sts 2 days before the NACH Deduction Date instead of 5 days as per earlier practice. Moreover the practice of opening internal tickets for NACH stop need to be stopped.Tickets opened in Monthly Mode Enquiry for NACH Stop will be marked as "Invalid Ticket" even tickets opened 1 day  before NACH deduction Date and will not be entertained by NACH team.Example: NACH deduction date :05 May,2019. The NACH can be stopped by  BM/CCM on or before 03 May,2019. Any ticket which is raised on 04 May,2019 in Monthly Mode Enquiry bucket to stop NACH, will be marked as "Invalid Ticket"In case of any further query, you can contact vocteam@indiamart.com
