# On the basis of sellers' BL NI behavior we have started marking Negative Countries

Previously (consisted of only preferred countries):If at least 2 BLs purchased of that country in 1 year and at least 1 BL purchased from that country in last 90 days, then it is preferred country.Changed Logic:Fresh Country/Negative country to Preferred Country:If at least 2 BLs purchased of that country in 1 year and at least 1 BL purchased from that country in last 90 days & wrong location BL NI of last 90 days < 2*(BL Purchase of last 90 days) {if wrong location BL NI in last 90 days >= 3}, then it will be preferred country.Fresh Country/Preferred Country to Negative Country:If Wrong Location BL NI >= 3 in last 90 days and Wrong location BL NI >= 2*(BL Purchase) in last 90 days, then that country will become a negative country. It will remain a negative country unless it satisfies the preferred country condition.*Fresh country is country which is neither preferred nor negative country.
