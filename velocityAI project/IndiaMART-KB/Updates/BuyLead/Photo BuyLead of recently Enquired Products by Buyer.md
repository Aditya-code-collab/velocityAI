# Photo BuyLead of recently Enquired Products by Buyer

The BuyLead card is now enriched with details of products that the buyer has recently enquired about. The Products Enquired section contains the image, name and price of products for which the buyer has sent an enquiry recently.Benefit:This will enable sellers to make more informed decisions before consuming the leads. These additional details will help in filling the gap between what the buyer is actually looking for and what the seller has perceived.What has been done?On the BL cards on the BL search page, we are showing products with name, image, and price on which the buyer has sent an enquiry. Displaying additional details of products enquired by the buyer will enable the seller to make more informed decisions before consuming that lead.We have taken into account all those products against which enquiry was sent or any C2C activity was done by that buyerAll these products within a span of 24 hours before BuyLead was approved will be consideredPDN, Price and Image of the above products is displayed on BL Card (with up to 3 products to be shown)This is not to be confused with the Product of Interest shown in the buyer profile as those have MCAT names of previous BLs posted by the buyer along with the MCAT from search and browse activities of the buyer.
