# Stopped SMS for high volume sms receivers (Seller)

Sellers who have received more than 100 SMS in a month, we have disabled the privacy setting of enquiry related to SMS (Enquiry & PNS) from the back end and communication for the same has been sent through email. Also, we are showing a pop-up regarding this activity in their seller dashboard. This pop-up will reflect a seller while he is logged in his account.
