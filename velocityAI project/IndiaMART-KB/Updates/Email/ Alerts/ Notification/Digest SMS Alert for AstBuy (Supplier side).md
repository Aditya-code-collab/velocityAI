# Digest SMS Alert for AstBuy (Supplier side)

We send digest SMS alert for ongoing AstBuy.Please note that there is no change for INSTANT ASTBUY SMS.Problem/Issue: Suppliers were flooded with a lot of SMS by ASTBUY. Hence, there were very less chances of suppliers reading those SMSes.What we have done?We have optimized by sending 1 SMS/Day for ONGOING and PTT ASTBUY as a digest, (First digest on 12:15 PM) with existing checks - (a),(b),(c).Note - We'll consider both foreign and Indian leads in digest alert.  (a),(b),(c) conditions are applicable for Indian leads only.
