# Change in Supplier selection logic for BL Alert SMS for Indian Leads (30th May'19)

We have changed the supplier selection logic for BL Alert SMS for Indian leads. We have made the checks more strict to make it more relevant.Selection Logic Previously: 1. Supplier MCAT rank should be in A/B/D.2. Lead should be either supplier prime or Offer prime.3. No location based filtering was there.New Selection Logic : 1. Supplier MCAT rank should be A.2. Lead should be both supplier prime and Offer prime.3. Lead should be from one of the following locations:- Offer city and supplier city is same(Home City)- Offer city is supplier's preferred city (Pref City)- Offer district and Supplier District is same(Home District)- Offer City is from Supplier's pref district.(City of pref District)This is done keeping in mind to minimize the SMS volume by sending the most relevant BuyLead alerts.*Please Note: From 15th July'19 we are sending SMS at frequency 1 SMS per user per day
