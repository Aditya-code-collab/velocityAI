# Email Bounce Management Process | Changes in Repeat Email Bounce Logic (2nd July'19)

Earlier:Repeat Bounce was considered when there was at least 1 qualified bounce in last 1-5 days and at least 1 qualified bounce in last 7-15 days.Now:Repeat Bounce will be considered when there is at least 1 qualified bounce between last 1-3 days and at least 1 qualified bounce in last 4-15 days.Qualified Bounce: Qualified bounce is when both the below conditions are met:No mail is being opened in last 15 days (0 Open Event)Ratio of Bounce event and total mail request is more than 0.5.For example: 7 emails were requested by IM for a user. Out of which, 5 emails got bounced in last 15 days and no mail was being opened. In this scenario, we will consider it as a qualified bounce as the ratio of bounce to request is 5/7 which is greater than 0.5 and also 0 open event.
