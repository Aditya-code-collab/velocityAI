# Upload Media files in Photos & Docs drive through WhatsApp

Now we will be able to upload files in Photos & Docs drive on the seller dashboard through WhatsApp (IndiaMART customer care number)Current Process:Currently, in multiple catalog enrichment processes, our executives get product images from sellers either on their personal WhatsApp number or IndiaMART WhatsApp numbers(other than 9696). Once they get the images on WhatsApp, they download it on their systems & add products using Seller or GLADMIN screen.What we have done:We have created a functionality wherein a seller can share their product images/docs through WhatsApp (IndiaMART customer care number) and it will get uploaded on their respective Photos & Docs drive on the seller dashboard in real-timeFile type for which this functionality will work as of now:ImagePDFWordExcelZipFollowing are the checks which we have implemented as of now:# If a user does not have GLID
