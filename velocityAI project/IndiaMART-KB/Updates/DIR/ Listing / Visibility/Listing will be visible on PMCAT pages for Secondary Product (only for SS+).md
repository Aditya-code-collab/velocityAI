# Listing will be visible on PMCAT pages for Secondary Product (only for SS+)

In order to resolve the DIR listing issues for premium clients where the primary product is not mapped in the client's mapped PMCAT. The recent changes have been done in logic.So, if the primary product is not mapped in PMCAT and only secondary products are mapped then also client's premium listing will be visible in PMCAT pages (All India & District)We are happy to inform you that we have added premium listing clients with only secondary mapped products to PMCAT All India & District pages for All PMCATs.This logic is applicable for only SS+ cases. For TS & MDC the logic will remain unchanged i.e., the listing will be visible on PMCAT pages if the primary products are mapped.Note: Premium Listing Sellers with only Secondary products will still be given a lower priority than other sellers with same cust type and primary product(s).
