# Caller wants to know supplier's registration process on IndiaMART. (FCP Creation)

If caller wants to register on IndiaMART as a supplier, educate that following details are required:- Owner's Name- Company name- Minimum of 3 products with images- Mobile number- Email ID- Company address with pin codeNote: For registration client should be a regular supplier. Ask the caller to search for seller.indiamart.com on the web browser and click on "New to IndiaMART- Join Now?" below the log-in section.
