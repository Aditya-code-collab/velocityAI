# Retention Script: Business Closed

To-Dos:Check whether GST is active or not.  Check the last GST filing status to discuss.Ask the Client: Were the BL/enquiries we delivered not relevant? If there is issue of irrelevancy, follow the rebuttals accordingly. If he says: Shifting abroad/town: Sir, will you be doing business there also? Educate how IM can help him get leads from all over the world.  He was an employee has moved to other company: Take the email address of the owner & who will be handling business now. Tell Benefits. Partnership issues: From today onwards, who will run this business?- If another partner, then need to talk to another partner Business is closing: If closing the business, convince the client to use services, and inform him that we can do required changes in catalogue and account details
