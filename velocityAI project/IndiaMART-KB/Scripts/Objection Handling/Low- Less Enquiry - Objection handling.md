# Low/ Less Enquiry - Objection handling

Ask the client if any product-specific enquiry is less or enquiry flow, in general, is quite less/no enquiries are availableIf the client is complaining about the enquiry of a specific product is lessAsk the client enquiry of which product he is looking forCheck whether that product/ MCAT is added or not in clients' catalogIf not, then add the same and also ask the client for other MCATs in which the product can be mapped, this would help in searchabilityMCAT Ranking: Check, how many products are under C rank MCAT. If product you are looking has C rank than it will get less priority in searchPrice on Product: If a paid supplier don't have price on his products then it will come below from the same segment of suppliers who have prices.If the client is complaining about low/ less enquiry irrespective of any specific productCheck through STS whether there is an actual dip in the enquiries. If not, then inform him the sameIf yes, then check the checkpoints
