# Outstanding Amount/ Partial amount not paid after the deal conversion

Deal Conversion (Outstanding Cases): Balance payment cases wherein the client is not willing/able to pay the balance amount (Concluded during Conversion ticket), within the stipulated time, mentioned below two scenarios can occur:1.       Service downgrade to Monthly Mode: Cases wherein the client agrees to get the services converted back into the monthly mode as he is not able to pay the balance amount.2.       Service downloaded, refund to be initiated: Cases wherein the client is not willing/able to pay the balance amount, supposed to be refunded on a pro-rata basis.Please do not raise an internal ticket in such cases, the refund shall be initiated by the outstanding team basis their internal process and in case of any specific queries/doubts/prioritization with respect to any case,  kindly mark an email to “outstanding@indiamart.com” keeping  “pankaj.kumar@indiamart.com” in the loop with complete details
