# Change bank account details for NACH

Customer requests NACH bank account change request (1st NACH hit pending/ 0-30 day customer)System Check: Check CL or Onboarding icon to confirm if this is first NACH hitResolution: Inform the customer that first NACH should be cleared from the registered bank account only, hence changes cannot be done before first NACH hit (OCR) Customer requests NACH bank account change request (Post 1st NACH hit)Resolution- Confirm reason of NACH bank change requests and issue a ticket with complete details.Identify the reason for account change.Check if the new bank account is mentioned in "accepted bank account list".For Contact Center: Raise a ticketFor Servicing Team (MDC clients only):The sales team need to generate an account modification link via WebERP. The user will get an option to change the account detailsWhen the new NACH is successfully registered, the existing proforma will be mapped to the new bank account. No new proforma will be generated.NACH gets activated in real-time with updated account details.
