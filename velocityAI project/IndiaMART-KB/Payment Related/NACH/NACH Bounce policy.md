# NACH Bounce policy

NACH bounce policy for 0-30 days tenure client- We will download the 0 NACH cleared customer if 2 consecutive Auto NACH hits get bounced. Such cases will be downloaded within 2 days of post receiving clearance status of 2nd hit.Example: If a client is hosted on 3rd Dec’18, his First Auto hit will be scheduled on 5th Dec’18 and second Auto hit will be on 10th Dec’18 presuming both the hits are bounce, we will download this client within 2 days post the 2nd NACH hit i.e. 10th Dec’18.Note: If the customer wants to enrol again in paid services, then he would be eligible for annual mode only.If 1st NACH is not cleared within the 14 days of hosting, the same will be processed for downloadIf the clients' NACH is bounced continuously 5 times then he has to take the annual mode packageIf the clients' NACH is bounced and it has increased its limit (10+1) then he has to take the annual packageIf there is three consecutive Nach bounce then the Nach will stop but the services will be continued. After this, we will ask the client to select NACH date (2 adhoc NACH are allowed- this is an internal process, do not share it with the client)Multiple bouncing- we will ask the client to select NACH date (2 adhoc NACH are allowed- this is an internal process, do not share it with the client) If still the NACH is bounced then we will download the services
