# How a supplier can ask for the rating?

Guide the Seller to follow the below steps to send an email to the buyer for rating individually via Lead manager section from seller panel.Step1: Login  to seller.indiamart.comStep2: Open Lead ManagerStep3: Select the Buyer to whom he want to send rating mailerStep4: Click on 'View More Details' Link available on buyer contact headerStep5: Click on "Ask for Rating" / "Request Rating" buttonAfter clicking on the "Ask for Rating" / "Request Rating" button, buyers will get an email with an option to rate the supplier.Buyer can click on the number of star (1/2/3/4/5 Star) from the email body.Buyer will be landed to My.Indiamart.com with an option to further add his rating comment and images also.This is one  of the most influential ways to get a positive rating.
