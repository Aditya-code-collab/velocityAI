# Features for Seller Rating

We provide the following features on seller rating:Any buyer who has interacted with a supplier post Matchmaking or successful PNS connect will have the option to rate the supplier (Interaction includes call attempts, negotiation, Text Replies from both side or completed deals)Buyer can rate the supplier between 1 Star to 5 Star.We have started displaying user satisfaction parameters (if available) at the top of the page which shows the seller how they have satisfied the buyers on basis of their responsiveness to buyer requirement, quality of products/services and delivery of the product/service.The supplier can sort ratings of the buyers' on the basis of recency, high to low, low to high, comment.The supplier can revert on the comment of the buyer.The supplier can also click on the name of Buyer so as to Directly Chat with the buyer on LMS (redirection available)
