# Supplier Rating on Seller Panel

What is Supplier Rating?Supplier Ratings are the ratings provided by a buyer to a supplier based on his experience while dealing with him. Buyers can rate the supplier from 1 to 5.Please NoteIf the client has taken the external website then the rating will not be visible in the catalog.(It is visible on tdw catalog)Who can Rate?Any buyer who has interacted with a supplier will have the option to rate the supplierInteraction includes call attempts, negotiation or completed dealsObjectiveTo promote positively rated suppliers and sensitize the negatively rated suppliersThis should encourage better buyer experience on IM platformRating CalculationA simple average is applicable till the number of reviews is limited to 10. Once sellers receive more than 10 ratings then the weighted average calculation is applied. This takes into consideration rating recency and overall performance.  Buyer Rating Mailer FrequencyThese are not a buyer rating mailer , but are overall Fulfillment mailers for indiamart . Rating is applicable only if the seller gets selected in the feedback mailer. The frequency is updated below.Buyer Feedback Mailer Frequency:Indian Inquiries : 5th day/15th dayIndian  Buy leads: 6th Day/ 15th DayForeign Buyers: 10th Day /15th dayHow do we capture ratings?Through Fulfillment mailers: where the buyers can share the status of their requirement and in case the buyer requirement has been fulfilled, he can go ahead and rate the supplier, his requirement has been fulfilled by.Through Message center: Any Buyer can use message center to rate sellers they have contacted.Through Review mailer: If a seller requests a review from a buyer, then the buyer receives a review mailer using which the buyer can review the seller.Benefits/ ImportanceSeller rating is a measure of the seller’s performance on IndiaMART. Benefits of rating:Help seller to build trustHelp to build brand and credibility Free word of mouth marketingAct as a feedback to improve business/ product/ serviceFAQsI have been incorrectly rated negativelySuggested Ans: The UI used to capture ratings from buyers is very crisp and clear, it is a conscious decision of the buyer because there might be something that he did not like about the dealI have not dealt with the buyer, why am I being negatively rated? Suggested Ans: We only allow Buyers to rate if we have any records of him interacting with you. So, please don’t worry. Anyone and everyone cannot rate you.The buyer deliberately rated the deal negative because he wanted a lesser price than what was finalizedSuggested Ans: We will take actions against a buyer if we receive multiple complaints against him.Would the negative ratings be displayed in my Catalog?Suggested Ans: All ratings are getting displayed on catalogs. We recommend you to earn maximum high ratings so that your Avg rating is improved.How can we contact the buyer?Suggested Ans: We do not encourage our suppliers to speak with rating providers so that buyers’ privacy is maintained.How can a buyer change the provided rating?Suggested Ans: Buyer can now edit the rating of the supplier. He needs to select the supplier whom he wants to change the rating. It will have an edit option nearby the rating.Does the buyers' rating impact the listing of the supplier?No, Currently, buyers' rating does not impact the suppliers listing.
