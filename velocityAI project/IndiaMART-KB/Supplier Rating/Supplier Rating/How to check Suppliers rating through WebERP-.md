# How to check Suppliers rating through WebERP?

Seller's reviews and ratings can now be viewed in the Activity chart in WebERPOverall rating and Parameter-wise rating is displayed separatelyReviews given by individual buyers with the details of each rating is also displayedSorting of reviews is available on the basis of:Recency Review (High to Low)Review (Low to High) Reviews with commentsPath:Open WebERP >> Open desired company >> Go To Activity >> Click on suppliers' Review
