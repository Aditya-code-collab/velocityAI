# What are Catalog Views?

Understanding Catalog Views on IndiaMARTWhat are Catalog Views?Catalog Views occur when a buyer visits and explores a seller’s product listings on IndiaMART—either through search results or the seller’s catalog page. These views indicate buyer interest and can be valuable leads for sellers.Where Can Sellers See Catalog Views?Sellers can find details of buyers who have viewed their products directly on the Buyleads page. Earlier, there was a separate Catalog Views page, but to simplify and enhance the user experience, all such buyer interactions are now available under one unified section—Buyleads.How to Contact Buyers Who Viewed Your Products?Sellers can reach out to buyers who have shown interest in their products by:Navigating to the Buyleads page.Identifying the lead under Catalog Views.Consuming 1 Buylead balance to unlock the buyer’s contact details and initiate communication.This ensures that sellers can instantly act on buyer interest and convert it into business opportunities.Why Was the Separate Catalog Views Page Removed?To streamline the seller experience, we have merged the Catalog Views into the Buyleads page. This provides:A centralized dashboard for all buyer inquiries.Ease of contact from a single platform.Improved lead management, helping sellers respond faster and smarter
