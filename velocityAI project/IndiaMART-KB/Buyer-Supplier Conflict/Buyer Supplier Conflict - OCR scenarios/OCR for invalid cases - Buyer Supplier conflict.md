# OCR for invalid cases - Buyer Supplier conflict

For complaints which are more than 1 year old concernsSir/Mam, We do understand your concern, but IndiaMART can not interfere much as you would have complained when incident occurred.  You are advised to get the issue resolved at your end only by coordinating with Supplier.  For Complaint where complete product received but issue raised related to invoice, bill etc.Sir/Mam, We do understand your concern, but I hope you can also understand that IndiaMART is not involved in any kind of deal related negotiations done between Buyer and Supplier, so we can not help much in invoice/bill or agreement related matters. As many things are decided by two parties mutually in which IndiaMART can not intervene.You are advised to get the issue resolved at your end only by coordinating with Supplier.
