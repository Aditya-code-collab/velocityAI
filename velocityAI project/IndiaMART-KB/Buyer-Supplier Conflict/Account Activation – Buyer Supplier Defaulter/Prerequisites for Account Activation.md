# Prerequisites for Account Activation

One can view all the complaints of sellers via WebERPPath:  Weberp > Company > Company > (Enter the GLID) > Cust Voice>Select (Parent-Child, All related, Against Me) to check the BS TicketsBelow are the prerequisite points to be considered while raising an Activation Request:Total unique BS tickets should be <=2 and <=4 in the last 1 year as free and paid users respectively.No BS Conflict ticket should be Unresolved or in WIP (including All Related Accounts).In case any BS ticket is in WIP then do not raise an activation ticket until the WIP ticket gets closed.In case any BS ticket is closed under the below dispositions i.e. Seller's Listing Delisted/ Buyer's Listing Delisted/ Defendant Is Already Disabled, then inform the seller about the unresolved BS Ticket first.Avg. Buyer-Supplier Conflict ticket TAT should not be more than 30 days.KYC to be attached (ID Proof/Address Proof-Non GST/Cancel Cheque/GSTCertificate/Office Board Image).If Getting Re-On boarded as a Paid supplier, follow Re-on boarding SOPAny ticket issued without full KYC/Proof of old BS Conflict resolved will be marked as NAT immediately without any intimation.
