# Conditions for a valid Buyer Supplier Conflict ticket

What are the pre-Conditions for Issuing a Buyer-Supplier Conflict Ticket?User against whom we received a complaint, should be an IndiaMART registered memberIf the complaint is against the buyer, he must have been received product from the supplierIf the complaint is against the seller, he must have been received some advance from the buyerIf the product delivered by supplier is defective and its poor quality is identifiable by IndiaMARTIf any other committed service/product (as per agreement) not deliveredAny claim made by the complainant (buyer or supplier) must have a supportive written documentIn which conditions a Buyer-Supplier Ticket will be considered as NAT or Duplicate?Conditions for NAT (Not a Ticket)If there is no monetary transaction between Buyer and SupplierWhen the complainant is unable to provide details of users against whom he wants to make a complaint (i.e. respondent)If the previous complaint (for the same deal between same parties) is concluded that we can not interfere in the said matter (Like quality issue, no proof claim available, action is already taken, deadlock situation etc.)If the complaint is related to more price charged by the supplier then market priceIf the deal is less than 1 year oldIf the deal is related to commission (or a third party is coming into the picture without any written evidence)Conditions for DuplicateIf there is already one ticket in W.I.P against him & he raised the ticket for his Account Activation.
