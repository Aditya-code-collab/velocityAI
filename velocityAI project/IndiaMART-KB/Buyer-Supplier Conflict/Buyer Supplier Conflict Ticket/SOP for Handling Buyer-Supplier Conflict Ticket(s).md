# SOP for Handling Buyer-Supplier Conflict Ticket(s)

Purpose:The purpose of this policy/sop is to ensure timely, proper and error-free handling of all the BS conflicts arising between Paid & Free Supplier and Buyer.This will ensure an increase in resolution % hence increased buyer and supplier satisfaction %Paid  User:Any registered member of IndiaMART having any active paid service running (i.e. MDC and above)Free User:Any registered member of IndiaMART having who is freelisted on IndiaMARTComplainant:- The user who did not receive the payment (or received only partial payment) from the buyer- The user who did not receive product (or received partial product) after making payment to the supplier- The user who received defective product- The User who Approached IndiaMART to get his issue resolvedNote: The deal should not be >=1 year old.Respondent:The user who did not deliver the product to the buyer or the user who did not pay the amount to the supplier even after taking the delivery of the product.
