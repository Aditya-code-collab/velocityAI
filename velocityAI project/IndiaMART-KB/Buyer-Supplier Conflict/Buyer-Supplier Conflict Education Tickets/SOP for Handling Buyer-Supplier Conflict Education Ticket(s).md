# SOP for Handling Buyer-Supplier Conflict Education Ticket(s)

What is Buyer Supplier Conflict Education?It is about educating users (especially our paid customers) of IndiaMART on what steps/precautions he needs to follow to ensure good service for his customers/buyers Need to educate the supplier/users with few tips and guidelines which he needs to follow while dealing with his customersIt is the process of analyzing past complaint against the supplier/user (if any) and educating user that what precautions he needs to take to avoid such complaints in future. When this ticket will be opened?1. Source 1:(a) When receiving 1st BS Conflict Ticket against the user i. This ticket will be opened by Buyer-Supplier Conflict Management team ii. Whenever Buyer supplier conflict Management feels that this supplier needs education based on his past complaint history related to Buyer-Supplier Conflict(b) In a few cases, repeat ticket can also be opened (if re-education required)2. Ideally, it will be opened for all the users having >= 1 Past Buyer-Supplier Conflict Ticket Steps to work on Buyer-suppliers conflict education tickets1. Users having >= 1 Past Buyer-Supplier Conflict Ticket. Please follow the below steps-a.  Analyze the past ticket(s) that what was the cause of ticket/complaint against himb.  Educate the supplier on below points           >> Quotation and Invoice must have detailed terms and conditions like  >> Specification of product >> Expected Delivery time >>    Refund and Return Policy >>    Transportation and insurance cost >>    Official Bank accounts and Official contact number(s) >>    Transportation damage policy >>    Advance % required to dispatch the product >>    Installation charges >>    Warranty and guarantee applicability and duration >>    Order cancellation policy in case of delayc.  Services may be impacted if we will receive any negative feedback in future (with politeness and in a proper way)d.  Send an email to users about your discussion including the above TIPse.  Document the same in Ticket and close the ticket under a relevant disposition 2.  Users having 0 Past Buyer-Supplier Conflict Ticketa.  Same as above point no 1.b.  i 3. Repeat Buyer-Supplier Conflict Educationa.  In a few cases, the ticket will be locked/ opened to re-educate the customer/ client/ supplierb.  Education is required to clients with past education reference and recent complaints if any 4.
