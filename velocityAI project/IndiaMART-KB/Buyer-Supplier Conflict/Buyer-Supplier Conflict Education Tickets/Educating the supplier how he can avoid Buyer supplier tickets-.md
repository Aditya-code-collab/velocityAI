# Educating the supplier how he can avoid Buyer supplier tickets?

In order to enhance our customers' experience, we have come up with someguidelines:1. Always ensure to share quotation/invoice with all the customers. It shouldinclude: Specification of product(s)/service(s) Refund/Return Policy in case of delay/quality issues or cancellation Advance(%) required to dispatch and estimated delivery time Transportation/Insurance/Installation cost2. Mention an official bank account on every quotation/invoice.3. Share your Helpline Number and make sure to answer every call.4. Kindly update your customer in case of any delay in the deliveryNote: Any repeat complaints against the suppliers' company may impact your ratings,goodwill or may interrupt your services temporarily or permanently
