# Add/edit Company Logo

Guide the steps to add/change Company Logo from Seller dashboard:(Seller.IM >> Company Profile >> Business Profile >> Company Logo)Educate the client about guidelines for Company LogoIf the client requests IndiaMART to add or make changes on his behalf:- For Contact Center: Inform the customer to share the logo which needs to be added at "customercare@indiamart.com"- For Servicing: Inform the customer to share the Logo via email and raise a self ticket to make the required changes.
