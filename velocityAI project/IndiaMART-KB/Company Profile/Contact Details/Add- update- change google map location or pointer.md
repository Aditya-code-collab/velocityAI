# Add/ update/ change google map location or pointer

Troubleshooting StepsCC Team1. The client complains that his location pointer on Google Maps through Indiamart catalog is wrong - Ask the client to share the location coordinates to be updated on Google Maps.2. The client complains that his location pointer on Google Maps is correct, but the address shown is wrong.- In this case, the client has to be told that we are just updating the customer location link as provided to us on the IndiaMART Catalog, which is further diverted/forwarded to a Google MAP. If the address on Google Maps is different from the customer registered address, then we cannot control/change this. This is because maps.Google.com is completely owned by Google and address for the specific Location Pointer on Google Maps are managed and geocoded by google. FOS team1. When the meeting Updated (on the Primary Address only) is showing more than 1 km from the updated Address: - Open a ticket for "Location Correction" along with the location to be updated. 2. When the meeting is done at the secondary address: - No need to open a ticket in this bucket.Pre-requisites for raising ticket in Location correctionAll the locations to be updated have to share through the ticket only, any location shared via Mail/ WhatsApp or any other means shall not be entertained.Before opening a ticket in this bucket, the following things are required, 1. For the CC Team The coordinates of the location to be updated are required.2. For the FOS Team FSF team can share anything out of the following listings while opening the ticket. ▪ Location of the meeting ▪ Board Image/Sign Board ▪ Visiting Card of the companyA note for the FOS Team: FOS team only needs to open the ticket when the meeting is done at the primary registered address and the system reflects meeting is beyond 1 km of range or the client is complaining that his location on the catalog is wrong. Any ticket in this bucket shall not be entertained if the meeting is done at secondary or some other address. In such a case, the location shall not be updated. For Contact Center TeamRaise a Ticket along with the location to be updated. For any queries, please reach to Shubhi Singh (singh.shubhi@indiamart.com)Manager-  Bejjavaram Rojaswi
