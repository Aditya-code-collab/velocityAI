# Add/edit alternate or secondary email id

Seller PanelGuide the client to follow the below path to add or edit alternative/ secondary email id from Seller Panel: Seller.IM >> Company Profile >> Contact DetailsIn case the client wants to update the additional email:Guide the client to click on the pen icon next to the alternate email address.Enter the 4 Digit OTP received on primary mobile number.Enter alternate email id which needs to be added.Enter the OTP received on alternate email id (Newly Added).Upon successful verification, alternate email id will be updated.Mobile App (IOS and Android)Ask the client to open the mobile app and follow the below steps:Click on three horizontal lines on the top left Click on the pencil Icon, It will direct you to the edit profile pageScroll down and add the details in Other InformationAdd/ Edit the Alternate Email IdEnter the OTP received on Registered NumberVerify the OTP received on the alternate email idUpdate and savePlease NoteFor OTP issues kindly contact the concerned person
