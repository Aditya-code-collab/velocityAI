# Swap "Primary and Secondary Email ID"

Guide the client to follow the below path to make changes from Seller Panel:Seller.IM >> Company Profile >> Contact DetailsRemove the secondary email address from click and save option.Enter the 4 digits OTP received on your primary mobile number.The secondary email will be removed on successful verification of OTP.Click on the pen icon next to the primary email address.Enter the 4 digits OTP received on your primary mobile number to allow modification.Replace the email id mentioned in the primary email field with the email of your preference.Click save and enter the OTP received on your mobile followed by the OTP received on your email.Upon successful verification, the primary email address will be updated.Add the email you want to update as secondary email in the alternate email field and click save.Enter the 4 digits OTP received on mobile followed by 4 digits OTP received on email to update the same.
