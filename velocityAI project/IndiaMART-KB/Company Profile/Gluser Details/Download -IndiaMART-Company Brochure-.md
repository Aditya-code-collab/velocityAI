# Download "IndiaMART/Company Brochure"

To download the "IndiaMART/ Company Brochure" ask the client to follow the below-mentioned steps:Login to "seller.indiamart.com" using that company's credentials.Click on entity's name appearing in the blue hyperlink (Company name) on the top left-hand side of the homepage.Hover the mouse on the "About Us/ Profile" tab on the right-hand side of the screen.Select "Download Brochure" option at the bottom of the list.
