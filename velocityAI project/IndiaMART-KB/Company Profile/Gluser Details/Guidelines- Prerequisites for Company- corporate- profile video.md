# Guidelines/ Prerequisites for Company/ corporate/ profile video

Company Video Guidelines:Minimum 6 products should be live clients' catalog.Open an Internal ticket in Media galleryIt is mandatory to share video initially while opening the ticket, the attachment should be shared in WebERP and if an attachment is big then share using google driveAlways share ticket ID with an attachmentVideo size should not be more than 5 MBIM team doesn't upload videos of Pharmaceutical customersIM team can't make the changes (Editing) in Customer video, the customer has to make the changes if requiredBefore sending the video, Customer care representative should check whether video matches with customer details or not.Only videos hosted on YouTube can be accepted.Content of the video should not be copied and should belong to the client's company. Also, get the approval mail from the customer saying that shared video belongs to the customer and that he/she allows IndiaMART to add it on the IndiaMART YouTube channel & it should be shared within 24 hours of opening the ticket.Mail Format to be UsedDear << Customer Name >>,Greetings from IndiaMART!We request you to kindly share any original promotional content that you might have in the form of Images, Videos, Brochures etc. so that we can use this for the better promotion of your organization. Please note that the content should be an original piece of your work and should be your copyright. We also request your confirmation authorizing IndiaMART to use your content like text, pictures, videos etc. for promotional and other purposes on all IndiaMART platforms like website, app, Facebook page and YouTube channel.Kindly reply to this mail with your confirmation.Thanks If video is related to the product only or demonstrates the production process, then it should ideally be added under the product video section on the seller dashboard. For product videos, ask the customer to share YouTube URL for the same or ask him to upload it on his YouTube channel and share the URL with CC or suggest the below path to add product videoSeller Dashboard >> Manage Product >> Go to the desired product of which you want to add product video >>  Click on Add video >> Paste the video link/URL >> SaveApproval of the product video typically takes 48hours, after which the video starts showing on the seller’s catalog. If the video is rejected, the customer will get a rejection mailer citing the rejection reason and the video will not be displayed on the catalog.
