# What is the difference between Maximiser and MDC?

The Maximiser and the basic service, Mini Dynamic Catalog,  differ on the following criteria:MDC runs on subdomain while Maximiser runs on own domainMaximiser comes with TrustSEALMaximiser has multiple templates while MDC has a single templateIn the Maximiser package, you are provided 30 India BuyLeads per week + 2 daily bonus India BuyLeads, whereas you get only 7 India BuyLeads per week + 1 daily bonus India BuyLeads in the MDC package.Click here to check our customers' help section for this article.
