# How to enable 2 step verification (Gmail)?

https://www.google.com/landing/2step/Please follow these steps post clicking on the link -Click on Get StartedClick on Get Started (Next Page)Enter your official Email Address and PasswordEnter your Mobile Number and select Text Message option and click Next  (Please enter mobile number which is available and working)Enter OTP/Code received on Entered Mobile NumberClick on TURN ON* at the endPlease don't forget to click on TURN ON at the end.
