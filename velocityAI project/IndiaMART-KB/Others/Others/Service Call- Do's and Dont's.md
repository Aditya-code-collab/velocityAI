# Service Call: Do's and Dont's

The Dos and Dont's of Service CallWhen you are talking to the customer, please ensure that you keep in mind the following points-Prerequisites: Keep a pen and paper ready before making a call to the customer. Opt for Virtual Meetings: Check if the client is ready for Whatsapp call / Skype calls / Zoom callsAssess the availability of the customer: Don't be pushy if the customer doesn't want to talkBegin the call by stating its purpose which entails IndiaMART's exclusive ability to help your business even in lockdown.Emphasize on the allocation of 30% extra BuyLeads.Explain the reason behind the increase in BuyLead allocation -The physical business has come down and buyers are looking for supplies online. With a lot of buyers coming online, there is an increase in IndiaMART buyer traffic & the number of leads.You can contact & fulfil buyers' requirements and make the maximum use of it by using these extra buyleadsLeverage this to build strong relationships with these buyers and further develop your sales funnel.    Educate the client about the need for daily activation, quickly follow up with the buyer as a buyer posting a lead at this time will have an urgent requirement. Educate the client about Pay With IndiaMART option which can help him resolve any payment issues.  Listen to the client's concerns - Make a note of all the issues and concerns mentioned by himAddress all his issues properly by giving point to point explanation to all the concerns raised by him with the correct objection handling.  Close the call by assuring that IndiaMART is fully operational & available for your service as it used to be. Let us deliver quality service to our valued customers.
