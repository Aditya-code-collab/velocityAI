# HRS (High Risk Suspected)-Tag Removal

What is High Risk Suspected Users?These are those users having some association with a fraud marked (or fraud suspect) companies. This is created to alert all of us that we should review the case to ensure clean marketplace.When this tag appears?This tag/popup appears when account (User) is found associated with any other company (or self) which is disabled under non-compliance (Like Fraud Complaint, Fraud Suspect, Multiple BS conflict Tickets etc.)For most of the cases, High-Risk Suspected User Tag will appear on MERP/WebERP top Bar (Be informed that in few of the cases, you might get such popup without any tag of HRS on top bar)It is advisable to open the ticket and get the tag removed before approaching the user for any kind of Payment / Payment Receipt entry to avoid bad user experience in case of refund to be doneWhat is the Pre-Requirement before locking Ticket?User is enabled (not disabled)HRS Tag or Popup is coming in WebERP/MERP while Call/Receipt/OVP/Production updateWhat is the step to lock the Ticket?For WebERP Users kindly follow the below path:Open STS >> Open Ticket >> High Risk Suspected Tag Removal >> UpdateEnter your comment in detail about HRS removal requiredWhat is Expected TAT for closure?Expected TAT is 3-7 working days (Monday-Friday) in normal scenario (For complicated cases, it might be increased).Reason for delay: As BS conflict team need to make follow up with buyers to know about old pending issues resolved or notAfter confirmation of HRS tag removal from the HRS Team, it might take 24 hours to get it reflected in WebERP for few casesIf we found any Receipt entry / OVP / Production is blocking due to this, you are advised to call the below-mentioned employee after opening the ticket to get it evaluated immediately to avoid any further delayEscalation MatrixLevel -
