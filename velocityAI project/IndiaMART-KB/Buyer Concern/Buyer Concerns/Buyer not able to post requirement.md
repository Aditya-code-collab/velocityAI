# Buyer not able to post requirement

Requirement posted by buyer can be auto deleted due to following reasons (auto deletion process get processed by cron job every day at 10 PM )Check if buyer is posting lead from Blacklisted IPBuyer is posting BuyLead from Blacklisted countryBuyer catalog has been marked as fraudBuyer’s past 3 leads were deleted on call leading to auto deletion of new posted leadLeads posted by the user who are marked as disabled user in IM’s database are auto deleted.If the email posted by the user if of the domain present in the invalid email domain list of IM, that lead is auto deleted.In a case where buyer’s one lead is already live, and he posts another lead of the same Prime MCAT as that of live lead, the second lead posted is auto deleted.If the buyer has at least one waiting lead and the same user posts another lead within 3 mins of generation time of the first lead, then we auto delete the first lead of the user.
