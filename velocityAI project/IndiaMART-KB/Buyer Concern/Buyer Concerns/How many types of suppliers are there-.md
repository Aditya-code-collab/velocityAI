# How many types of suppliers are there?

IndiaMART has broadly two types of suppliers listed on its platform.Paid Suppliers- They are IndiaMART clients who avail our valuable services like a catalog, listing, and PNS (Preferred Number Service) services. Free Suppliers- These are suppliers listed on IndiaMART platform with a free catalog page (FCP).Note: Sellers’ listing depends on the frequency of their buyer responses and their past history of dealing with buyers.Click here to check our customers' help section for this article.
