# Is there an option to compare catalogs?

No. Currently, we do not provide the option to compare seller catalogs and prices.However, you can individually evaluate products and their prices in the sellers’ catalog.The sellers can mention either a static price for the products or a range of the price he is willing to sell the products for based on the order size. The buyers can negotiate with the seller directly in case he wants to purchase the product in bulk.Click here to check our customers' help section for this article.
