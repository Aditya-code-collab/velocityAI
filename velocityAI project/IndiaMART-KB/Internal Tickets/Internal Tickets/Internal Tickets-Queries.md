# Internal Tickets/Queries

OverviewInternal tickets (also called Internal Queries) are raised by the CC/FSF teams to get specific backend tasks completed by internal departments such as:FinanceProductionMarketplaceGST TeamAccountingProduct/Technical TeamsInternal Queries are categorized into:Customer Oriented Internal QueriesCore Internal QueriesCustomer Oriented Internal QueriesWhat Are Customer Oriented Internal Queries?These queries directly impact the customer journey.Whenever a Customer Oriented Internal Query is raised, it must be linked to an existing External Ticket or Self Ticket.If none exist, the system will auto-create a Self Ticket.How to Raise Customer Oriented Internal QueriesMethod A — Through STS Update (from GL ID)Steps:Login to Weberp → Search the GL ID → STS → Update → Open Ticket → Customer Oriented Internal Queries → Select Correct Bucket → Select/Link External or Self Ticket (or system will create one)VideoMethod B — Through Any WIP Customer TicketSteps:Open the WIP Customer
