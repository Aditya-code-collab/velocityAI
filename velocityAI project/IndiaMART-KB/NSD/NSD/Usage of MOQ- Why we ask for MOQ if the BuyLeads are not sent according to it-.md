# Usage of MOQ/ Why we ask for MOQ if the BuyLeads are not sent according to it?

MOQ (Minimum order quantity) is asked from the client so that the same can be mentioned in his catalog. It might eliminate few of the buyers who have requirement less than MOQ.  If the buyer reads the MOQ mentioned by the supplier then there is a possibility that the buyer do not enquire him if the requirement is a mismatch.
