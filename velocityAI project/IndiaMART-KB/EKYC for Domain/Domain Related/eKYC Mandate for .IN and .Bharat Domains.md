# eKYC Mandate for .IN and .Bharat Domains

Overview The eKYC (Electronic Know Your Customer) mandate is a crucial step towards enhancing the security and integrity of .IN and .Bharat domain registrations. This process ensures that domain holders are verified individuals or entities, reducing the risk of fraudulent activities and promoting a trusted online environment.  This document outlines the eKYC process, timelines, eligibility criteria, and other important information. Timeline: 15 Days Following the trigger event (detailed below), domain holders have a 15-day period to complete the eKYC verification process. Failure to complete the verification within this timeframe may result in temporary suspension or other actions as per the domain registry's policy. It is crucial to start the process immediately after receiving the notification. Eligibility & Restrictions The eKYC mandate applies to all new .IN and .Bharat domain registrations and may be triggered for existing domain holders as well (see 'Trigger Events' section).  Certain restrictions may apply based on the domain type (e.g., individual vs. organization) and the information provided during registration. Ensure that all details submitted during the eKYC process match the original registration information. Trigger Events The eKYC process may be triggered under the following circumstances: New Domain Registration: Upon successful registration of a new .IN or .Bharat domain. Domain Transfer: When transferring a .IN or .Bharat domain from one registrar to another. Change of Ownership: When there is a change in the ownership details of the registered domain. Random Audits: Periodic random audits to ensure compliance and maintain data accuracy. Suspicious Activity: Any detection of suspicious activity related to the domain registration. Step-by-Step Verification: Digio 3-Stage Process 1. The domain holder will be notified to rectify the issues and re-submit the verification. ❌ Temporary Block If the eKYC verification is not completed within the stipulated timeframe (15 days), the domain may be temporarily blocked. To restore the domain, the eKYC process must be completed.  Continued failure to comply may result in further action. Reminder Logic To ensure timely completion of the eKYC process, automated reminders are sent to domain holders: Initial Notification:  Immediately after the trigger event. Reminder 1: 7 days after the initial notification. Reminder 2: 12 days after the initial notification. Final Reminder: 14 days after the initial notification, before temporary block. Escalations For any issues or concerns regarding the eKYC process, domain holders can escalate the matter by contacting our support team via email or phone.   Please provide your domain name and a detailed description of the issue for prompt assistance. Email: customercare@indiamart.comPhone: +91-9696969696Summary This document has outlined the importance of the eKYC mandate for .IN and .Bharat domains, including the process, timelines, eligibility, and potential escalations.
